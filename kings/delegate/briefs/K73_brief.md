Referee brief, 2026-11-25. Output in `kings/delegate/out/K70/verify/`.

## What is claimed

Lemma B (statement and rules of the game P: `kings/delegate/briefs/K70_lemmaB_blind.md`, the only brief the author
saw): White to move, Black Kb6, pb7, pc6 against a White king and one pawn on c2, c3 or c4; Black wins in P for
every White king square not next to b6, except Kb4 with the pawn on c4, a draw. A model of another vendor, working
blind, wrote `kings/delegate/out/K70/PROOF.md` (1,035 lines): an opening rule for Black's first reply, Table B
(486 rows, Black to move: Black king square, zone of White king squares, the move to play, and for every legal
White reply a lower-numbered row), Table D (97 rows, White to move, for the one drawn start) and a blockade lemma
F. It labels the whole PROVED "by descending induction" and says a program was used only to find the rows.

Why it matters: Proposition 4.25 of `kings/theory/LOOPY.md` is proved by hand except for Lemma B (CHECKED-2, three
programs). It uses 42 of the 162 positions (`kings/delegate/out/K68/verify/VERIFY.md` §5).

## Check

5. **The part the proposition needs.** Starting from the 42 positions of VERIFY.md §5 and the opening rule, list
