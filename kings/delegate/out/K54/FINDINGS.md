**Method.** `pawns_check.py` computes the any-order first-passer threat colours per coordinate, then tests HSS and (E) throughout the stopped whole-board reach. A failing root HSS test suffices to exclude the placement. A right fails (E) only at a nonterminal multicomponent node with an actual e.p. capture. The sum of remaining distances per colour bounds every later sum of stopped tempo counts, so verifies (B). The comparison is between `cgt.outcome(pawns.value(...))` and a separate ordinary alternating minimax with one flag and immediate terminal scoring. Both use the project's pawn moves, passedness and count distances. That shared code is why the census has the label COMPUTED-1. Integer-constructor memoization only reuses interned CGT values.

**PROVED reduction; COMPUTED-1 finite conclusion.** The tables settle the omission of (E) for right-free 3+3 roots **on these board sizes and settings**, including roots with a single component. Here is the coverage argument.

1. A live component has at least one pawn of each colour. With five files, any disconnected arrangement in which (E) can ever fail has two groups of widths 2+1, 3+1 or 2+2, up to reflection and moving empty separators. Three groups would all be single files, and a single-file group cannot acquire an e.p. capture. The same holds for two singleton groups. Empty separators persist; enlarging a gap, reflecting the board or moving the outer empty files has no effect on either game within the groups. On a five-file cylinder every disconnected two-group arrangement requires two gaps and thus embeds in the same flat representatives.
2. While a position stays connected, both engines play exactly the same moves with one current right and test the whole moved component before splitting. A first split requires a capture that empties a file, and hence leaves **no new right**. If the split move creates a passer, both engines score it immediately. Otherwise the child has at most five pawns and inherits HSS and the bound from its parent's any-order hypotheses.
3. At five pawns after the first split, the only possible material from 3+3 is 3+2 or 2+3. We also enumerated both materials in all three width families. Their combined counts per rule at height 6 were 528 with (E), 168 failing (E) later and 1,728 unsafe. At height 7 they were 2,180, 420 and 6,400 respectively.
