K40 FINDINGS -- Gafni's K+P vs K values without edges (LOOPY section 8 item 5). Not refereed.
Hand proofs: PROOF.md. Proposed LOOPY edit: EDITS.md (applies cleanly; OLD occurs once).

0. SUMMARY
- In LOOPY's D-model a lone K+P vs K has no value: the pawn is a passer, so the position is scored as a
  decisive event (BIG) from the start. The meaningful object is the P-model component x:
  - a promotion is a White win;
  - Kxp leaves K v K, which is dead (PROVED, Lemma 1).
  The "value" of x is its results in x (+) T over 17 terminal-free test games T, with each side to move:
  - the integers -12..12 (n > 0 gives White n spare moves);
  - on, off, dud, over, under, *, up, down and +-1.
  A test-game move is illegal while in check.
  Two conventions for a side with no move:
  - (N) normal play: that side loses (LOOPY's D-semantics);
  - (C) chess: checkmate loses, stalemate draws. (C) is P itself.
- CHECKED-2, the bare P table: kpsum, kp_p --pmodel and ref3m agree at all 1,056,504 legal positions,
  with 0 differences. Boards: cyl:3-8x6 and cyl:3-8x8.
- Sums: kpsum.c and ksum2.py agree, 38,971,824 results with 0 differences, under (N) and (C).
  - Both codes are K40's own, so this is two implementations by one author.
  - The integer chain runs to +-12 on 3-6x6, 3x8 and 4x8; +-6 on 7x6 and 5x8; +-4 on 8x6 and 6x8; +-2 on 7x8 and 8x8.
- Atlas vs P: the atlas code disagrees with P at 54 to 8,264 positions per board.
  - Every disagreement is P=W and atlas=D.
  - All of them come from the atlas scoring a promotion that allows Kxq as a draw. Stalemate-after-promotion
    cases occur only on 4 files (8 per board).
  - With P's promotion rule substituted, the atlas code reproduces P with 0 differences.
  - Every disagreement is listed in data/atlas_disagreements.tsv.xz.
- Under (N), on 3 and 5-8 files every (x, s) is one of three classes:
  - DEC: W against every test;
  - ON: the signature of on (and of on+*);
  - DUD: D against every test.
  No number, no infinitesimal, no {on|0} and no 1* occurs. on* cannot be told apart from on by these tests.
  Gafni's reported values therefore do not appear. This is against a second-hand summary; the thesis was not located.
- Under (C), on 5-8 files, ON splits into tempo classes. These include parity classes, whose results are not
  monotone in the integer tempo n, so they have no CGT value.
  - PROVED: Ka6 Pa7 ka8, Black to move (W >= 4), wins for White iff Black has an odd number of spare moves (n < 0, n odd).

3. THE RULE
(N), 3 and 5-8 files (COMPUTED, cross-checked by the two sum codes):
- DEC = P_L = W(x (+) dud).
  PROVED (Prop. 4): DEC is contained in W(x (+) T) for every terminal-free T. This is LOOPY Theorem 3.11, whose
  proof survives the check rule.
- ON = W(x (+) 0) \ P_L: W against every integer -12..12, over, under, *, up, down and +-1; D against off and dud.
  White needs no spare tempo, and 12 Black spares do not help Black. COMPUTED only; no hand proof.
- DUD: D against everything, on included.
  - On 3, 6 and 8 files (H = 6, 7, 8) and on cyl:7x6, DUD is exactly "Black forces Kxp against a White who may pass at
    will". With Kxp scored as a Black win in x (+) on, no draw is left.
  - Exceptions: fortress draws on odd widths, with both kings at cyclic file distance (W-1)/2 from the pawn.
    Counts: cyl:5x6 30, 5x7 100, 5x8 170, 7x7 182, 7x8 378 (example: cyl:5x6 Ka1 ka3 Pc3, White to move). COMPUTED.
- The class is fixed by two bits: the normal-play KPK table (x (+) 0) and x (+) dud. The normal-play table has
  W set = DEC u ON and D set = DUD.
- Placement pairs (White to move / Black to move): only DEC/DEC, DEC/ON, DEC/DUD, ON/ON, ON/DUD and DUD/DUD occur.
- PROVED (Lemma 2, Cor. 3): White is never stalemated when W != 4, so Black never wins x (+) T. Hence no {on|0},
  no negative numbers and no off.
- 4-file cylinders are exceptional, as in K6:
  - finite thresholds theta in {-4..1}; "other" counts 76+132 (4x6) and 212+268 (4x8);
  - 28 Black-win positions per board, where White is stalemated in front of the pawn (e.g. Ka8 Pa7 kc7).

(C), which is P:
- On 3 and 5-8 files, DEC and DUD are the same sets as under (N).
- cyl:3xH: all of ON becomes DUD.
- cyl:5-8xH: ON splits as follows.
  - Few P-ON positions (8x8: 1856 w, 680 b).
  - Monotone thresholds: W iff n >= theta, theta in {-1, 0, 1}; -1 only for H=8. Example: 8x8 Ka1 Pa2 ka3 w has
    theta = 1: a P draw, won with one White spare.
  - Parity classes (8x8 examples):
    Ka2 Pf2 kb4 w: W iff n >= -1 or n odd;
    Ka3 Pg4 kb5 w: W iff n >= -2 or n even;
    Ka6 Pa7 ka8 b: W iff n < 0 and n odd.
- The parity classes are not monotone in n, so they equal no loopy normal-play game (Lemma 1.6).
- PROVED (Prop. 5): the family K(f,H-2) P(f,H-1) k(f,H), Black to move, W >= 4. It is exactly the set with the pattern
  "W iff n < 0 and n odd" on every board run with W >= 4 (COMPUTED).
  Mechanism: a stalemated Black must spend any spare he has. White's three pawn-guarding king squares form a path, so
  White cannot lose a tempo. This is LOOPY Remark 1.8 from the tempo side: Gafni-type values need "stalemate loses".
- The P table does not determine the class: on 8x8, the ON positions with White to move are 19,504 P wins and
  15,528 P draws.

4. ATLAS VS P (every disagreement in data/atlas_disagreements.tsv.xz; counts in data/atlas_diff_counts.json)
Reasons:
- Kxq: White promotes now, and Black's king takes the queen.
- stalemate: the promotion stalemates; 4 files only, 8 positions per board.
- later: P's win passes through such a promotion further on.
Counts, White to move (Kxq + later) and Black to move (later):
- 8x8: w 784 + 2656; b 4816 + 8
- 6x6: w 252 + 252; b 810
- 7x6: w 378 + 532; b 1652
- 8x6: w 528 + 912; b 2512
The disagreeing positions lie in DEC, except a few Black-to-move ON positions (8x8: 8; 7x6: 126). No atlas W is a
P draw. These are (A_Q) failures (LOOPY section 1.2).
