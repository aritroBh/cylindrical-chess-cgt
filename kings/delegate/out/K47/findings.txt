K47 FINDINGS: the K45 over group Z_O = Kf5 kd4/kd5 Pb2 Pb4 Pe2 pa4 pb5 pe5 (cyl:8x6): hand proof, and its value in sums
================================================================================================================================
Task date 2026-11-23.  AI work, no human re-derivation.  Model P (SPEC.md rules, e.p. on, the first promotion wins for the promoter,
stalemate and endless play draw; LOOPY Definition 1.5).  Labels: PROVED (hand proof, PROOF.md), COMPUTED-1 (one implementation),
CHECKED-2 (two independently written implementations agree).  Root A = Black king d4, root B = Black king d5.

0. SHORT ANSWER
---------------
(1) Hand proof (PROOF.md), PARTIAL.  PROVED: the move lists of the four roots and the two freezes; q_A (the freeze e2-e4 at root A) is a
    mutual zugzwang of P: Black to move, all 4 moves lose ((O3) at A); White to move, all 6 moves lose ((O4) at A); so root A has a
    freeze.  The proof uses three race lemmas (White's e-pawn behind a king on d5/d6; Black's e-pawn after e5-e3 behind a king on d3 or
    d2; the a/b-pawn race after b2-b3) and holds on every cyl:Wx6 with W >= 8.  NOT proved by hand: (O1) at both roots, (O3)/(O4) at q_B,
    and that the other White moves at the roots lose (so that e2-e4, e2-e3 are the only freezes).  The solver's lines for those are
    11 to 25 plies deep; the exactness of Z_O as a whole stays CHECKED-2 (K45), now with a fifth solver (za, below) agreeing.
(2) Z_O is NOT equal to over in D-sums.  Against the 275 test games T of the brief (integers -6..6, on, off, dud, *, up, down, +-1, over,
    under, and all 253 sums of two of them), both roots, both sides to move, normal play (Definition 1.2): r(Z_O + T) = r(over + T) at
    1,062 of 1,100 results and differs at 38 (CHECKED-2: implementations A and B agree on all 1,100 results and on all 1,100 over results).
    In every difference Black does better with Z_O than with over (Z_O gives a Black win where over gives a White win or a draw); never
    the other way.  The differences come from T = *, down, under and sums containing them (list in section 2).  Example: root A with
    Black to move beside *: over + * is a White win, Z_O + * a Black win, by 1...e5-e4!, a move that loses in P alone (after 25 plies,
    through zugzwang) but wins when Black keeps * in hand.  The two roots are not even equal to each other: they differ against 8 of the
    550 (test, side) pairs (*, down and five sums).  So Z_O is an exact group of Definition 4.8 whose sum value is not over; on these
    tests it lies below over (r(Z_O + T) <= r(over + T) for White in all 1,100).  Its value was not identified.
(3) P contexts.  On cyl:8x6 there is no file at distance >= 2 from the group (files a, b, e; kings on d and f; free files c, g, h, and g
    touches f, h touches a, c lies inside), so the context test was run on the smallest wider boards where K45 found the group exact:
    cyl:9x6 with the context on file h (buffers g, i), all 63 contexts of at most 2 + 2 pawns: the four P results agree with r(over + C)
    in all 63 (ref3m and kp_p --pmodel agree on all 252 positions; CHECKED-2); cyl:10x6 with files h, i (buffers g, j), 200 of the 885
    contexts of at most 2 + 2 pawns (seeded sample): 199 agree, 1 differs (ref3m and kp_p agree on all 800 positions; CHECKED-2):
    `Ph2 pi3 pi5` beside root A, White to move, is a Black win, though r(over + C) is a White win (root B: White win, as over).
    The D-sum r(Z_O + C) of the same contexts (A, COMPUTED-1) differs from over in 4 of 63 and 5 of 200 contexts (all at root A); P
    follows the D-sum in only 1 of these 9 and over in the other 8.  So in P the context test hardly separates Z_O from over, while the
    D-sums do (section 3a).

1. IMPLEMENTATIONS (item 2)
---------------------------
Component.  Z_O as a component of a D-sum is the P game of the group's own pieces (K40's convention for a P position x in x + T): every
placement reachable from the two roots by moves of EITHER side (in a sum either side may move twice in a row in the group), a promotion is
a terminal win for the promoter and ends the whole play, a side in check may not move in T (checkmate loses), a move in T clears the
group's e.p. right, a side with no move at all loses (N, Definition 1.2) or draws (C).  3,914,687 placements, 21,661,215 White and
22,029,008 Black moves (both implementations count the same nodes).  Its bisimulation quotient still has 3,397,278 classes (src/zq.cpp),
so there is no small game graph to read a value from.
  A  src/za.cpp: bitboards, hashed placements, explicit forward lists, one retrograde pass with counters over (placement, T-position,
     side), predecessors from reversed lists; over + T by its own small fixed point.
  B  src/zb.cpp (written separately): mailbox board, base-5 packed keys, a generic component interface (the chess group and over are
     both components), the product states reachable from the start found first, then the two attractors (White wins, Black wins)
     computed separately.
Checks.  A's P values (T = 0, convention C, which is P itself) equal ref3m (K23) on 66,997 sampled states of the component (every 97th
node, both sides; 10,980 with an e.p. square; W 28,319, L 36,544, D 2,134; 0 differences; logs/sampleP_A_vs_ref3m.tsv.xz).
A's move counts and values at the 8 positions of Definition 4.8 equal K45's.  A and B: the 15 small tests in both conventions
(logs/small_*), 0 differences; all 275 tests in convention N (logs/full_A_N.out, logs/full_B_N.out), 0 differences (the output files
are identical).  Run times under heavy.sh at nice 15: A 8 min, 1.7 GB; B 47 min, 2.4 GB (B was restarted once after the 12:13 reboot).
Convention C is not a test of over: 0_D has no move, so over + 0 is a draw under C, while Z_O's freeze q is a real position whose moves all
lose.  The comparison with over is made under N only.
