K49 FINDINGS -- the ON class of K+P v K on the cylinder (LOOPY section 8 item 5). Not refereed.
Hand proofs: PROOF.md. Proposed LOOPY edits: EDITS.md (3 edits; apply_edits.py on a scratch copy: 3 applied, 0 rejected).

0. SUMMARY
- Everything K40 computed about the three classes under (N) (W != 4) follows by hand from ONE table fact:
  Lemma P (no reciprocal zugzwang): if White wins the bare normal-play game with Black to move, she also wins with White to
  move (Black not in check).
  - Lemma P is the -1 column of K40's tables, so it is CHECKED-2 on K40's 12 boards (cyl:3,5-8 x 6,8).
  - K49's own solver kn.c checks it on every cyl:WxH, W in {3,5..10}, 4 <= H <= 10: 0 violations (COMPUTED-1).
  - It fails on every 4-file cylinder (8..28 violations per board): this is where K40's 4-file anomalies come from.
  - PROVED by hand for the pawn on rank H-1. No hand proof lower down (the gap).
- Given Lemma P, PROVED by hand (PROOF.md):
  - Theorem 1: r(x (+) n) = r(x (+) 0) for EVERY integer n (K40 had |n| <= 12). Black's spares never help, White needs none.
  - Theorem 2: for every terminal-free T, a W_N position with Black to move wins x (+) T whenever Left wins on (+) T with Right
    to move; with White to move likewise, or via a first T-move if the Black-to-move twin is also in W_N.
    Covers every Right-finite T (all integers, *, up, down, +-1, under, on, on+*); over needs no Lemma P.
  - Proposition 3 (no Lemma P): if t lies in a Right reserve (off, dud, ...), every position outside P_L draws.
  - Theorem 4 (trichotomy): DEC = P_L, ON = W_N \ P_L, DUD = complement of W_N (D against every T).
    So the RULE for ON: White wins the bare normal-play game, but not against a Black who may pass.
- Pawn on rank H-1: an explicit king-distance rule, PROVED, 0 exceptions on 463,616 positions (W 3,5..9; H 6,7,8).
- ON is not the single game on: there are two types, (on, Left to move) and (on, Right to move). PROVED counterexamples to
  "the signature of on against every T" on cyl:8x8 with T0 = {0 | off}.
- DUD: forced Kxp against a passing White => D against every T, PROVED. The converse (no fortress) is COMPUTED only.
  Fortress geometry sharpened; new board cyl:9x8 has 522 fortress positions; none on 7x6, 9x6, 9x7.

  White to move: q empty or wk on q -> DEC; bk on q: wk within one king step of Pi (the 3 squares below p) -> ON, else DUD.

  PROVED on rank H-1; COMPUTED-1 on random T on 9 boards (none of the "neither" kind).
  Observed: ON/ON with White to move -> (on,L); ON with Black to move and ON twin -> (on,R); Black in check -> (on,L);
  twins DEC/ON (Black to move) and ON/DUD (White to move) split.
- Proposition 8 (forced Kxp against a passing White => DUD): PROVED.
- DUD = forced Kxp on 3, 6, 8 files; the odd-width fortresses: COMPUTED (counts 30/100/170/182/378 reproduced; 9x8: 522).
  Geometry: kings on the two back files (cyclic distance (W-1)/2 from the pawn); White to move: same back file; Black to move:
  different back files (except 10 on 5x7 and 20 on 5x8, same file, rank distance 5); rank distance >= 2 always.
  Mechanism: heuristic only (odd W has two adjacent back files, so Black's king can mirror White's).

3. NO SHORT RULE BELOW RANK H-1
- Two ranks from promotion the ON set has 103 / 135 relative configurations (White / Black to move) for W >= 7 (73/115 on 5,
  88/125 on 6), identical for H = 6 and 8. A table, not a rule.
- The feature vector (side, pawn rank, king offsets, side of pawn, king file distance) separates the classes with 0 conflicts on
  every board, but it is the whole position up to symmetry (25,563 vectors on 8x8).
- So the checkable rule offered is Theorem 4's: two table look-ups (bare normal-play game; game against a passing Black).
