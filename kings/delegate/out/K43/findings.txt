2. WHAT AN OVER GROUP IS IN P, AND THE TEST
-------------------------------------------

2.1 The D-game. over = {0 | over}: White (Left) has one move, to the zero 0; Black (Right) can pass for ever (a loopy option to over itself).
    Its mirror is under = {under | 0}: White passes for ever, Black has one move to 0. In P a "pass for ever" is a king shuttle and the move to 0 is a
    *freeze*: a move after which the opponent, to move, is in zugzwang and loses. LOOPY Definition 4.8 (K26) defines the exact UNDER group (White shuttles,
    Black freezes); the exact OVER group is its colour mirror (ranks flipped, colours swapped, side to move swapped). Restated for over, so that nothing has to be
    mirrored in the reader's head:

    EXACT OVER GROUP (P). Black's king shuttles on an adjacent pair z0, z1 (K1: both squares are empty and not attacked by a White pawn). White's king stands on one
    square w0 (K1: empty and not attacked by a Black pawn; K2: at king distance 2 or more from z0 and from z1). There are four roots (z_a, w0, side to move). Then
      (O1) with Black to move at either root, every legal move except the shuttle z_a -> z_(1-a) loses at once (the position after it is a White win), and the shuttle is legal;
      (O2) with White to move at either root, White has at least one legal move that does not lose at once (a FREEZE), and no freeze is a promotion;
      (O3) after every freeze m, in the position q_m (with the e.p. flag if m is a double step), Black to move has a legal move and every legal move loses at once;
      (O4) q_m with White to move (no flag) is a legal position, White has a legal move and every legal White move loses at once.
    So q_m is a mutual zugzwang (a zero), and with the shuttle as the pass the group is {0 | over}. By Lemma 4.11 (PROOF.md) every Black move at a Black-to-move root loses at once,
    the shuttle included, so the test does not depend on which move is called "the shuttle". Dropping (O4) gives a *loose* over group. In P the root values of an exact over group are:
    White to move wins (a freeze), Black to move loses; this is why plain P, not a draw-valued local game, is the right reading for the exact test (K26; LOOPY Conjecture 4.7, "two readings").

2.2 The computable test (`plocal.cpp`, function testUnder with shuttler = Black; the under test is the same function with shuttler = White). For every pawn
    configuration (layer), every adjacent pair z of safe squares for the shuttler, every safe square w0 of the freezer's king at distance >= 2 from both: look up the P values
    of the children of the four roots in the table of the layer and its sublayers (the table holds EVERY placement of both kings, so every king move and every pawn move of a
    root is a table look-up, the e.p. children are evaluated on the fly). It is the two-shuttle test of K15/K26 with one king's shuttle replaced by a single freeze move:
    the dud test (both kings shuttle, every non-shuttle move loses at once at all 8 roots) is the same loop with the freezer's side also shuttling (`--mode dud`).
    The funnel counters of the output are: placements with K1 and K2; F1 = (O1) at both Black-to-move roots; F2 = F1 + (O2); F3 = F2 + (O3), the loose groups; F3one = F3 with exactly
    one freeze at each root; F4 = F3 + (O4), the exact groups.

5. WHY NOTHING IS FOUND: WHAT THE DATA SHOW
-------------------------------------------

What fails is always (O4)/(U4), never (O3)/(U3): of the loose groups (F3), every one fails U4 (failChk: the freezing pawn gives check, so q with the freezer to move is illegal; failLeak: the freezer has a move
that does not lose at q). Typical: after the freeze the freezer has a WINNING move at q (a king step or a pawn push; diagnostic `--diag`, 3 files, 3+3, cyl:7x6: of 64,155,605 freeze records 63,631,549 have a legal q and
only 122 of them no winning freezer move; 114 placements have at least one freeze that leads to a true zero, "someZero"). So "all White moves lose at q" almost always comes with "Black wins at q whoever moves", the opposite of a mutual zugzwang.
Half-exact placements (cyl:7x6, 3 files, 3+3, both orientations): in 40 of 4,419,901 loose placements one root is exact (every freeze gives a zero) and the other is not. In all 40 the freeze of the good root is a single pawn push, which
is legal at the other root and loses there at once, as Lemma 4.10 says it must (checked on 38 roots with the shuttle legal in q: 0 violations; 2 roots have the shuttle illegal in q). With exactly one freeze at each root there are
10 placements with one zero root and one bad root (and 5,774 with two bad roots; none with two zero roots):
   Kb5 kg4 Pa4 Pc2 pa5 pc3 pc5 (shuttle b5-a6 or b5-b6), Kg5 kb4 Pa4 Pc2 pa5 pc3 pc5 (g5-a6, g5-g6), Kb5 kd4 Pa2 Pc4 pa3 pa5 pc5 (b5-b6, b5-c6), Kd5 kb4 Pa2 Pc4 pa3 pa5 pc5 (d5-c6, d5-d6),
   Kg5 kb5 Pa3 Pc2 pa5 pc3 pc5 (g5-g6), Kd5 kb5 Pa2 Pc3 pa3 pa5 pc5 (d5-d6)   [cyl:7x6; list in `near1_cyl7_f3_33.txt`]
All ten were re-checked with ref3m (`verify_roots.py`: the zero root has one freeze with U3 and U4, the other root one freeze with U3 and not U4; CHECKED-2). In each the shuttle partner is on the top rank.
The structure these show: Black has one freeze that is good against the king on one shuttle square and loses against the king on the other (Lemma 4.10); an exact group needs a second freeze with the opposite property,
and the search finds none. This is a description of the data, not a proof of non-existence. Funnel facts: on cyl:7x6 (4 files, 3+3) 123,380,174 of the 150,558,022 placements that pass U1 are lost because the freezer has a promotion at one of its roots.
No parity or opposition argument is known that excludes the second root; Lemma 4.10 is the structural fact that was proved.

----------------------

- More than 3 + 3 pawns on four files, five or more files (a 2-bit table for 5 files, 3 + 3, cyl:8x6 needs 1.3 GB and was queued but not run), more ranks (the program takes any H; checked against ref3m on cyl:8x7 and cyl:8x8, 600 positions each, but no
  exhaustive run was made), widths above 9 and flat windows on files not covered above. The runs of `run_ext.sh` that did not start before the end of this task are not claimed.
- Officers and chess (kpsolve was not run: no candidate exists). The free-pass reading of K15 (structural over against free passes, draw-valued local game) was not re-run; K15's ranges stand (cyl:7x6 and 8x6, four files, <= 3+3, 0 over).
- Groups whose closure contains White pawn moves, a freezer king with its own safe moves, or a pass-like tempo made of a pawn: not covered by the exact test and not by Lemma 4.11.
- Non-existence is not proved. The remaining gap is exactly a second freeze (section 5).
