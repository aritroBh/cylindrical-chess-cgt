1. WHAT WAS RUN
======================================================================================================================

Game.  K20's local game LK (kings/delegate/out/K20/summary.json; LOOPY section 8 item 3, "Model"): both kings move, blocked
pairs (white pawn on rank r, black pawn on r+1, r = 2..H-2) never move, a king may capture a pawn, a capture that leaves
exactly one side with a passed pawn is a terminal for that side, a player with no move loses.  Value = outcome pair in the
D-sum with the battery B34 of K20 (34 named games).

Boards.  cyl:WxH with H = 6 and W = 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 (the 64-square limit of kg44 stops at 10x6); extra runs below for
W = 11, 12 (and 13, 14, 16 for the over configuration) and H = 7 (over configuration only).

Configurations.  1, 2 or 3 pairs, on DISTINCT files, all inside an arc of at most 6 consecutive files ("window 6"; "window 5"
= arc length at most 5).  Ranks 2..4.  Up to rotation and reflection of the cylinder.  The file sets inside windows 5 and 6
that K20 (window 4, two pairs) did not cover are all included; every configuration is run, faithful or not.  "Faithful" is
kg.cpp's own test (every reachable non-terminal set of pawns has no pawn push and no pawn capture), exactly K20's flag.

Regions (both conventions of K20).  free: the whole board.  box(m), m = 0, 1, 2: kings confined to the minimal arc of files
that contains all pawn files, widened by m files each side.  When several arcs have minimal length (ties) each arc is its own
region.  Boxes that reach the whole width are the free run and are not repeated.

Totals.  2,131 (configuration, region) cases (60 with one pair, 396 with two, 1,675 with three; 793 faithful: 60, 282, 451);
1,756,517 root entries, 710,182 of them in faithful cases (root = king placement with all pawns alive, listed once per case).

Baseline.  K20's own enumeration (at most two pairs, files within 4) re-run with kg44 on cyl:6..10 and flat:6..8, free and
boxes m = 0, 1, 2: 967 distinct B34 signatures on cylinders, 676 on flat boards, 1,137 together.  The free runs reproduce the
referee's distinct-root counts of K20 (cyl:6x6 14,804, cyl:7x6 22,313, cyl:8x6 31,397).

Implementations (three, written by three different authors).
  kg44   = K20's kg.cpp with three flags added (--cfgfile, --fsig, --faithonly); scripts/kg44.cpp.
  lk     = the K20 referee's lk.cpp, unchanged (scripts/lk_ref.cpp); regions given as F<lo>-<hi>.
  lk3.py = new Python solver written for K44 from the definition (scripts/lk3.py): own game builder, own bisimulation
           quotient, own retrograde D-sum solver; used on W = 6, 7, 8 and for single roots.
Agreement.
  kg44 against lk: all 2,131 cases, for every named class count, the count of unclassified roots and the number of
  distinct unclassified signatures: 0 differences (scripts/cmp_lk.py); and the SET of distinct unclassified B34 signatures of every
  case is identical: 66,518 signatures compared (lk's context order and outcome coding translated), 0 differences (scripts/cmp_lk_sigs.py).
  kg44 against lk3: W = 6 (196 cases) 0 differences; W = 7 (288 cases) 0 differences; W = 8 (429 cases) 0 differences.  (Box regions repeat per width: a box that does not wrap is the same game on every W > its width, so the 2,131
  cases are not 2,131 different games; the free runs are different for every W.)
  Signature level, third implementation: all 206 new signatures that occur in a faithful case were recomputed by lk3 from a root of
  a faithful case: 206 of 206 agree (scripts/verify_new.py faithful).  Of all 1,909 new signatures lk3 recomputed 1,625 (the job was
  stopped when the machine was needed for the final steps; 0 disagreements); the other 284 rest on the kg44-lk set comparison above
  (two implementations, CHECKED-2).
  Extended batteries for over (the claim "this root equals over" is a statement about these batteries only):
    B4553 of K20 inside kg44: over agrees 2 of 2 roots (and all other named classes of the configuration agree);
    the referee's 23,716-game battery inside lk: over agrees 2 of 2, disagree 0, all other classes agree;
    lk3: over and under roots against over/under plus every D-sum of at most two B34 games (629 contexts, White and Black to
