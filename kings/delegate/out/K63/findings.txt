Meaning of a cap-1 result.  At cap 1 at most one officer may be on the board.  A promotion that would make a second officer is
"capped": bound A scores it as a White win, bound B as a Black win (the refinement scores a capped promotion by the favoured
side as the value after the opponent's best capture of the new piece; sound for that bound).  A >= chess value >= B (White's
view).  So: where A = B the value is the exact value of the position in chess under kpsolve's rules (SPEC.md: no 50-move
rule, no castling, endless play is a draw, stalemate a draw); this is a proof by computation, CHECKED-2 here.  Where A > B
the position is undecided: (W, L) says nothing, (W, D) says Black cannot win, (D, L) says White cannot win.  A cap-1 bound is
never evidence about what happens with two or more officers; no move is "refuted" by an open bound.  The 50-move rule could
in principle turn a long win into a draw; not checked.

4. QUESTION 3: F2 BESIDE OTHER PAWNS IN P (one board, one game)
---------------------------------------------------------------
Setting.  One board (flat 8x8), one game P: F2's pieces plus context pawns X on free files; F2 at a root (A or B) or at a q,
either side to move, no e.p. right except qA with Black to move (b3, unusable).  Prediction: the D-sum result, in which X is
the D-game of the context pawns alone (pawn moves only, single and double steps, captures, e.p.; a promotion is a terminal
win for the promoter; a side with no move loses; in the sum a move elsewhere clears X's e.p. right).  By Corollary 4.17 (on
flat 8x8, section 1) r(F2-root (+) X, s) = r(omega (+) X, s) and r(q (+) X, s) = r(X, s), so the prediction is
r(omega (+) X) at the roots and r(X) at the q's.
Separation (Definition 2.1).  F2's kings and pawns stand on files a, b, c, f.  The files at distance >= 2 from {a, b, c, f}
(and from the interval a-f) are h only; d, e and g are next to a group file and are buffer files.  So only contexts on file h
are separated in the note's sense; contexts on g touch the f-pawns (f5xg6, ...f6xg5 are possible), contexts on d, e touch
the Black king's files and the f-pawns.  The separation is about pawns: the kings of F2 may walk anywhere in P.
