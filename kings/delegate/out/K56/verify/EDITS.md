**Definition 4.12 (pass games; tempo-exact over groups; new, K56, 2026-11-23).** For a colour X and k ∈ {0, 1, …, ∞}, P^X_k is P on the group's pieces in which X may pass at most k times (k = ∞: at will): a pass is allowed only when X is not in check, leaves the board unchanged, gives the move to the other side Y and clears the e.p. right; Y never passes; a side with no legal move and no pass allowed is stalemated (a draw); P^X_0 is P. A move of X *loses against k passes* if Y wins P^X_k after it. An exact over group (Definition 4.8) is *tempo-exact* if (T1) no king is in check at the roots and at the q's (this follows from Definition 4.8 with K1, K2) and, at the roots only: (T2) every non-shuttle Black move m loses against one Black pass, or White is not in check after m and m followed by a White pass loses for Black against one Black pass; (T3) every non-freeze White move m loses against unlimited White passes, or Black is not in check after m and m followed by a Black pass loses at once for White in P. Nothing beyond Definition 4.8 is asked at the q's. The stronger *pass-robust* form (every move that Definition 4.8 requires to lose at once, at the roots and at the q's, loses against unlimited passes of the mover) is also sufficient for Lemma 4.13: its proof runs unchanged with an unlimited budget for both sides (K56 referee). It need not imply tempo-exactness, because more passes do not always help the passer: a side with no move and no pass left is stalemated (a draw), while a side with a pass left must pass (a toy game shows this, `kings/delegate/out/K56/verify/src/mono.py`).

**Lemma 4.13 (tempo-exact groups have the results of over). PROVED** (new, K56, 2026-11-23; `kings/delegate/out/K56/PROOF.md`; one AI proof, re-derived step by step by the K56 referee, `kings/delegate/out/K56/VERIFY.md`; no human re-derivation). Let G be a tempo-exact over group, taken as the P game of its own pieces in a D-sum (Definition 1.3 with K40's convention, as in the K47 bullet of Conjecture 4.7). For every finite D-game T, loopy or short, with or without terminals, every position t of T and each side s to move: r(root ⊞ t, s) = r(ω ⊞ t, s) for both roots (ω the position of over_D, Proposition 4.6), and r(q ⊞ t, s) = r(t, s) for every q. *Proof idea.* The winner (or the side that does not lose) in over_D ⊞ T copies its strategy S while the group stays at the roots and q's (the shuttle is Black's pass ω → ω, a freeze is White's ω → 0_D); after an opponent's move that Definition 4.8 calls losing, it answers the opponent's group moves inside the group by its pass-game strategy, answers moves elsewhere by S's answer elsewhere, and makes an unforced group move only when S asks for an over-move. S can ask that never at 0_D, once for White (ω → 0_D) and any number of times for Black (ω → ω): these are the pass budgets 0, 1 and ∞ of (T2), (T3) and of the q's. Draws are transferred with non-losing strategies; a winner in check always answers on the board; terminals of T are handled by S, since the opponent's moves in T are always answered in T. With unlimited passes, letting the winner pass too does not change its winning set (PROOF.md, Remark 4). Tempo-exactness is sufficient, not shown necessary. *Sanity check (K56 referee, COMPUTED-1):* on abstract toy groups (finite D-games without checks or e.p. rights, `verify/src/toy.py`), 2,000 random tempo-exact groups have the results of over beside 1,500 random finite D-games (loopy, with terminals) at all 9,064,258 results (216 of them meet (T2) or (T3) only through the after-a-pass alternatives), while 543 of 3,954 exact groups that are not tempo-exact differ from over somewhere. *Computed:* none of the nine exact groups of K45 and K48 tried (Z_O on cyl:8×6 and flat 8×6, its two flat translates, the 2 + 3 group of cyl:10×6, the four groups of cyl:8×7) is tempo-exact or pass-robust exact, so **no known group meets the criterion** and the lemma has no instance yet. (T2) holds in all but Z_O and its translates, where 1…e5–e4 at the d4 root (and its translates) is saved by one Black pass, the K47 mechanism; (T3) fails in all nine, at White king or pawn moves at a root that one White pass, or unlimited passes, save. For Z_O on cyl:8×6 and the 2 + 3 group these root results are CHECKED-2 (K56's `src/pr56.cpp` and the referee's `verify/src/vg.cpp`, no shared code, agree on every root move of both groups; the P values of each agree with `kp_p --pmodel` on 1,350 and 800 sampled states); for the other seven groups they are COMPUTED-1 (pr56 only). So the lemma does not decide the 2 + 3 group, and **whether the 2 + 3 group is over stays OPEN**; beside 600 random D-games of at most 5 nodes (300 with terminals) it has the results of over at all 2,400 results, while the same games separate Z_O from over at 56 (COMPUTED-1, K56's own sum code, which reproduces K47's 936 Z_O results of test games with at most 20 nodes).
