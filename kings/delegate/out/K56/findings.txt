=================================================================================================================
AI work (one agent), no human re-derivation, not refereed.  Paths relative to kings/delegate/out/K56/.  Labels:
PROVED (complete hand proof, PROOF.md), COMPUTED-1 (one program), CHECKED-2 (two programs agree).
Model: as K47/K55.  A group is the P game of its own pieces as a component of a D-sum (Definition 1.3, K40's convention:
a promotion ends the play as a win for the promoter; a side in check may not move in the other component; a move elsewhere
clears the e.p. right; a side with no move in any component loses; infinite play is a draw).  over_D = {w, 0_D}, L(w) = {0_D},
R(w) = {w}.  Stalemate: a draw in P alone and in the pass games below; a loss in a sum only if the side also has no move in
the other component.

0. SHORT ANSWER
---------------
- Part B.  Lemma (PROVED, PROOF.md): a "tempo-exact" over group has the results of over beside every finite D-game T,
  loopy or not, WITH OR WITHOUT TERMINALS, at both roots and with both sides to move; each q has the results of 0_D (= T alone).
  Tempo-exact = Definition 4.8 plus, at the roots only:
    (T2) every non-shuttle Black move loses even if Black may still pass ONCE (N1), or loses even after a White pass in reply
         with Black still allowed one pass (V1);
    (T3) every non-freeze White move loses against UNLIMITED White passes (N-inf), or loses in P alone after a Black pass in
         reply, Black not being in check (V0).
  Nothing beyond Definition 4.8 is needed at q.  The brief's pass-robust form (every such move, at roots and at q, loses against
  unlimited passes of the mover) implies tempo-exactness, so it is sufficient too, but it is far stronger than needed.
  The budgets 0 / 1 / unlimited are exactly how often the proof's winner can be asked to make an unforced move in the group
  (White's only over-move is used once; Black's over-pass any number of times; at 0_D there is none).
  Draws need no separate treatment; two-sided passes (the winner may pass too) give the same winning sets as one-sided ones
  when passes are unlimited (Remark 4; not for finite budgets).  Sufficient, not shown necessary.
- Part A (COMPUTED-1, one program; its P values CHECKED-2 against kp_p --pmodel on 1,350 sampled states).
  All nine groups are exact in the sense of Definition 4.8 (reproducing K45/K48), NONE is pass-robust exact, NONE is tempo-exact.
  Black's side (T2) holds in every group except Z_O and its two flat translates (1...e5-e4 at the d4 root and its translates,
  saved by one Black pass: the K47 mechanism).  White's side (T3) fails in every group (a White king or pawn move at a root that
  is saved by one White pass, or by unlimited passes, and is not lost after a Black pass).
- The 2 + 3 group H10 (cyl:10x6).  The lemma does not apply ((T3) fails at five White root moves).  My own sum solver
  (written for K56, validated against K47's 936 refereed Z_O results) finds H10 equal to over on 600 random small D-games
  (300 with terminals) and on the K47 battery games of at most 30 nodes (sec. 3), 0 differences; the same random battery
  separates Z_O from over in 56 of 2,400 results.  So no counterexample; H10 = over stays OPEN (supported).

2. PART B: THE LEMMA (PROVED; PROOF.md)
---------------------------------------
Statement, sum operation, class of T: PROOF.md sec. 1-2.  Every finite D-game T (Definition 1.1), loopy or short, terminals
allowed; D-sum of Definition 1.3 with K40's convention; results by the least fixed point.  r(root (+) t, s) = r(w (+) t, s) and
r(q (+) t, s) = r(t, s) for every position t and side s.
Method: strategy transfer in both directions for winning and for non-losing strategies.  The winner Y copies its over-strategy
S while the group stays in {roots, q} (shuttle = Black's over-pass, freeze = White's move to 0_D); after a losing move of the
opponent X, Y answers X's group moves inside the group with its pass-game strategy, answers X's moves elsewhere with S's answer
elsewhere ("pauses"), and makes an unforced group move only when S asks for an over-move.  The number of such requests is the
budget: 0 at 0_D, 1 for White at w, unlimited for Black at w.  A move can also be read as the mover's own over-move (the (V)
alternatives).  Points the brief asked about (PROOF.md sec. 4): draws (both directions, no special case); a winner in check
(always answers on the board); different freezes at the two roots (irrelevant); one- versus two-sided passes (same winning
sets for unlimited passes); test games with terminals (covered: X's moves elsewhere are always answered elsewhere by S).
Weakest step (my judgement): the termination count in phase 2 (PROOF.md sec. 3, "Termination"), which combines the attractor
ranks of three pass budgets with pauses and the loss of e.p. rights; and (P2), the e.p. right, where the pass game's
"stalemate is a draw" differs from the sum's rule (handled by the b-safe invariant, which never needs a stalemated X).
What is NOT claimed: necessity.  Tempo-exactness is sufficient only; failing (T3) is not evidence against over.

3. THE 2 + 3 GROUP AND Z_O IN SUMS (COMPUTED-1; my sum code validated against K47)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sum code: pr56 --sum (product of the group's component with a test game, retrograde with counters, the D-sum rules above);
over_D (+) T by src/over56.py (a separate small Python solver).  Validation: Z_O against K47's test games of at most 20 nodes

Batteries:
  rnd_all: 600 random D-games with 2 to 5 nodes (src/gen56.py, seeds 1 and 2): 300 without terminals, 300 with W/B terminals.
  k47_le30: K47's 275 test games with at most 30 nodes (261 games).
Results (roots A, B, both sides; compare src/cmp56.py):
  Z_O beside rnd_all: 56 of 2,400 results differ from over (38 games; 9 of them with terminals), all in Black's favour
      (G = L where over = D: 37; G = L where over = W: 14; G = D where over = W: 5), as in K47.
  H10 beside rnd_all: 0 of 2,400 differ from over.
  H10 beside k47_le30: 0 of 1,044 differ from over.

(PROOF.md sec. 5), and no test game exploiting them was found.  H10 is not a stopper (K55), so no simplest-form argument applies.
H10 beside k47_le30: 0 of 1,044 results differ from over (261 games; agrees with K55, which ran these with other solvers).
