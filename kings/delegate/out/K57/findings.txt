0. SHORT ANSWER
---------------
- Proposition 1 (PROVED): over >= 0 in D-sums: White does at least as well beside omega as beside 0_D, for every finite
  D-game.  So Black, winning (or not losing) beside over, may give over's passes up at any moment.
- Lemma 2 (PROVED): an exact over group of Definition 4.8 has the results of over beside every finite D-game T (loopy or
  short, terminals allowed, both roots, both sides; each q has the results of 0_D) if (B1): every non-shuttle Black move at
  a root leaves a position that White wins beside -1 (Black may once move elsewhere).  Nothing beyond Definition 4.8 is
  needed on White's side or at the q's.
- Corollary 3: H10 = Kg4 kf6/kg6 Pb3 Pe2 pa5 pb4 pe5 (cyl:10x6) has the results of over beside every finite D-game.
  PROVED given CHECKED-2 computations (Definition 4.8: K45; (B1): K55's za and rz, refereed for cyl:10x6, and K57's pr57).
  Not refereed.
- Why K56 failed on White's side: it kept over's passes for Black after a bad White move and charged Black a board move for
  each.  Here Black answers the bad move on the board and then replaces omega by 0_D (Proposition 1); from then on Black
  never needs an unforced board move.  White's five "failures" of K56 (Kg4-f3 at both roots, e2-e4 at f6, e2-e3 and Kg4-g3
  at g6) need only that they lose at once in P, which Definition 4.8 gives.  Black's side genuinely needs one token, because
  White cannot give over up (that would be a downgrade for White): this is where Z_O fails (1...e5-e4), matching K47's
  observation that every difference of Z_O from over favours Black.
- No separating test game exists for H10 (by the corollary).  Sanity: the lemma predicts that the four cyl:8x7 groups, which
  satisfy (B1) by one program, are also over; K57's sum code finds 0 differences from over for three of them on K56's 600
  random D-games (sec. 3).
- Abstract check (sec. 3b): on the K56 referee's toy groups, the 1,758 random exact groups with (B1) never differ from over

1. THE PROOF (PROOF.md)
-----------------------
Sum operation, class of T, conventions: PROOF.md sec. 1.  Strategy transfer for winning and for non-losing strategies, both
colours.  Phase 1: copy over's strategy S while the group is at the roots and q's (shuttle = omega -> omega, freeze = omega
-> 0_D).  Entry after the opponent X's other move m (losing at once by Definition 4.8):
  E1 (X = White, anywhere): Black plays its P-winning reply; if m was at a root the virtual over becomes 0_D (Proposition 1);
     budget 0.
  E2 (X = Black at a q): White's P-winning reply; budget 0.
  E3 (X = Black at a root): White's reply in G with one Black token, by (B1); budget 1 (over may still ask White once for
     omega -> 0_D).
Phase 2: answer board moves on the board (attractor of the token game), moves elsewhere by S elsewhere ("pauses"), and make
an unforced board move only when S asks for an over-move (at most once, White, budget 1).  Termination by the attractor
ranks; draws transfer; terminals of T are answered by S.
Weakest step: the phase-2 bookkeeping (termination count and the budget, i.e. which side can be asked for an unforced board
move); for the corollary, the completeness of the computed list of Black root moves and the e.p. handling (below).
