Rule (Q3), CONJECTURE (empirical, from this table only).  Let dB = min(dA, dB) (the nearer shuttle square).
  (R1) White-favour failures occur in every pair with dW <= 3 (13 pairs, no exception); with dW >= 4 only in
       C125_05 h (dW 4), C145_15 h (7), C145_16 h (6, AUXW), C145_24 h (5, AUXW), C014_08 h (4, AUXL).
  (R2) Black-favour failures occur in every pair with dB <= 4, except C125_10 h (dB 3) and C014_04 h (dB 3, which
       fails in White's favour); with dB >= 5 only when dW >= 5 (no exception): C125_02, 03, 04, 12, 22, C145_12 h,
       15, 19, 23, 25 (10 of the 18 pairs with dB >= 5 and dW >= 5; two more, C145_16 h and C145_24 h, both AUXW, fail for White only).
  (R3) Hence a clean pair needs dW >= 4 and dB >= 5, with the one exception C125_10 h (dW 5, dB 3).  Conversely:
       dW = 4 and dB >= 5: 4 of 5 pairs clean (C014_06 g, C125_18, C125_20, C125_21; fails: C125_05 h, White's
       favour, 9 contexts); dW >= 5 and dB >= 5: 6 of 18 clean (C014_01 h, C014_06 h, C125_17, C145_13 h, C145_17,
       C145_20).
  So the simplest rule that holds: "a pair is clean only if the White king is at least 4 files and the nearer
  shuttle square at least 5 files from the context file (one exception, C125_10 h)".  The simplest sufficient rule
  tried, "dW = 4 and dB >= 5", has one violator (C125_05 h).  No rule in the two distances alone separates the
  pairs with dW >= 5 and dB >= 5: identical distances (dW 7, dB 5, Black king on c4/c5 or c5/c6) give 0 failures
  for C014_01, C145_13, C145_17, C145_20 and 2 to 5 (always Ph2 ph3, Ph2 ph4 first, F2's kind) for C125_03, 04, 12,
  C145_12, 15, 23, 25, C125_22: the group's pawns decide.  Two tempo counts were tried and do not separate either
  (section 3): static king-route lengths around the group pawns (src/routes.py) and the speed of each side's
  punishment beside a race game (src/race.py).
  (R1') A tempo refinement of (R1), COMPUTED-1 (pk_sum, 24 groups computed): let tau_B be, over the White king
       moves at a q that step towards the context file, the least r such that Black still wins after the move beside
       a White chain of r moves to a White terminal (how fast Black punishes the White king's departure; src/race.py).
       On every computed pair with dW <= 4 and such a move, White-favour failures occur iff tau_B >= 9: tau_B = 8 in
       the clean C014_06 g, C125_18 h, C125_20 a, C125_21 h; 9 or > 12 in the 10 failing pairs (C125_05 f, g, h,
       C125_06, C125_08, C145_09 a, b, C034_01 a, C034_02 h, C014_05 h).  This separates the cell dW = 4, dB >= 5
       (C125_05 h: tau_B > 12).  No such count was found for Black's raids (section 3).
  Reading of the rule: the White king within 3 files leaves its zugzwang and wins a Black pawn (K72's kind b);
  the Black king within 4 files leaves the shuttle and wins or ties down a White pawn (kind a); a far White king
  (>= 5 files) cannot come back to defend a blocked White rank-2 pawn (F2's kind).  dW = 4 is close enough to
  defend and too far to raid.
