K50 FINDINGS: kings/embed_check.py, a checker that certifies the frozen-king model for one K+P position
(brief: kings/delegate/briefs/K50_certified_embedding_checker.md; 2026-11-23)

SUMMARY
1. kings/embed_check.py (standard library only) reads a FEN, finds the king group Z, F_Z, the buffer and the
   context, checks separation (Definition 2.1) and reports CERTIFIED / FAILS / UNDECIDED for (V1), (V2), (V3), with a
   witness, plus the D-game result and what is proved (a result in P; chess only under (A_Q)(X), never checked).
2. Default (strict) mode uses only sufficient conditions stated in LOOPY. In it, the paper's two trebuchets
   (Fig. (a), (b)), Z_T and K29 class 1 are NEVER certified, beside any context, bare included: every sortie has a
   local win ((L1_P)) but the literal (L3_F) fails, as LOOPY section 5.1 itself records ("The strict form (L3_F) fails
   in every case"). Where a passer terminal can be reached, (V2) also fails to be certified, because (H2) of
   Proposition 2.11 fails for every trebuchet (each king stands next to a Z-pawn file). This is the honest answer
   to "is the frozen-king model certified": for the trebuchets, not by LOOPY as written.
3. --ctx adds two direct checks over an over-approximation of the reachable contexts ((L3) itself, and (V1b)
   via buffer squares). They are this program's argument, written out below, not statements of LOOPY. With --ctx
   the bare trebuchets and locked or slow contexts get certified.
4. Tests. (a) ChessSums: all 23 APART positions pass separation; all 14 ADJACENT and all 30 sampled SHARED
   positions fail it (with the declared group); none of the 23 is CERTIFIED (UNDECIDED, reason as in 2); the D-game
   result agrees with the listed outcome class in all 23. (b) 2,080 positions on 6-rank boards against
   kp_p --pmodel: 193 CERTIFIED (64 by Theorem 1.14(i), 129 by its sharp form), 7 FAILS, 1,880 UNDECIDED;
   0 CERTIFIED positions disagree with kp_p; all 7 FAILS are P draws.

USAGE (the paper side)
  python3 kings/embed_check.py [--board flat:8x8|cyl:8x8|flat:WxH|cyl:WxH] [--fz f,g,h] [--group Kf5,Pg4,kh4,pg5]
                               [--tmax 8] [--cap 300000] [--ctx] [--pretty] ['FEN' ...]
  - Default board flat:8x8. Positions are FEN/EPD (first four fields) or kpsolve tokens ("Kf5 Pg4 kh4 pg5 Pa2 w -");
    with no position argument, one per line on stdin. One JSON object per position on stdout.
  - F_Z: default = the shortest arc holding both kings' files. --fz sets it (needed when both kings stand on one
    file, e.g. K29 class 1: --fz a,b,c). --group declares the king group's squares (wk, wp..., bk, bp...); then every
    pawn on F_Z must be declared, else separation FAILS ("component pawn on a group file"). Use --group when
    rebuilding a test set from a generator that knows its group (ChessSums meta.json "freeze").
  - Read "verdict" first: CERTIFIED means the position may enter a test set whose ground truth is the D-result,
    and "proves" says which theorem gives it and for which side; "D_result" is that result. FAILS means a
    hypothesis is false with the witness given (separation, check, stalemate, a promoting Z-move). UNDECIDED means
    nothing is claimed; the clause entries say which sufficient condition did not apply.
  - The certificate is for the promotion game P (first promotion wins). For the chess value, also check (A_Q)(X)
    with kpsolve (Section 1.2 of LOOPY); the program never does.
  - Example: python3 kings/embed_check.py --pretty --board cyl:7x6 'Kb1 Pa2 Pb3 ka3 pb2 pc5 Pe3 pe4 w -' --fz a,b,c

WHAT EACH VERDICT MEANS AND WHICH LOOPY STATEMENT BACKS IT
separation (PASS/FAILS): Definition 2.1: one king each, no officer, kings not adjacent, no pawn on rank 1 or H,
  F_Z an arc holding both kings, buffer files (distance 1) pawn-free, so the context is at distance >= 2; nobody in
  check; H >= 5 (needed by Proposition 2.10(a) and the e.p. arguments). Also reports F_Z, buffer, context files and
  "files_left_for_components" (W - |F_Z| - |buffer|). If separation fails, nothing else is claimed.
Reachable contexts: every component of the context is enumerated by moves of either side in any order (an
  over-approximation of the chess-reachable non-terminal contexts; a double step yields both the state with and
  without the right; terminal = a passer under cylcgt's e.p.-aware test, which has fewer terminals than the textbook
  test, so the set is a superset for both). Cap 300,000 states, else UNDECIDED.
(V1)
  check     CERTIFIED: nobody in check at X; Proposition 1.15(a) (PROVED): check status is decided at X and no check
            arises before the first decisive event. A side to move in check makes separation FAIL.
  (E)       CERTIFIED: no reachable context carries a usable e.p. right, or only one-component contexts do
            (L1.6/Theorem 1.6 of notes/lemmas.md: (E) is about positions with >= 2 components). FAILS: a usable right
            at the root with >= 2 context components (Proposition 1.15(b)). Else UNDECIDED.
  (V1b)     CERTIFIED per side: a Z-move legal in every reachable context: a Z-pawn move or a king step onto F_Z
            (Lemma 2.2: context pawns never occupy or attack F_Z, and Z does not change before the first decisive
            event). FAILS: stalemate at X. (--ctx: also a king step onto a buffer square no reachable enemy context
            pawn attacks.)
(V3) per side, per Z-move m (computed in the Z-only position, a superset of the moves legal in context):
  FAILS if m promotes. CERTIFIED (with reply r and T) by Theorem 2.7(a) (PROVED in P): a promoting reply (T = 0, an
  immediate P-win), or a reply r with (L1_P) within T (local game of Definition 2.6: k = infinity passes, s-bar's
  king confined to F_Z, any s-bar promotion wins, an s promotion fails; Theorem 2.7 holds with (L1_P)), (L3_F)
  (every line where s-bar plays only winning moves leaves s a Z-pawn move or a king move onto F_Z), and (L2):
  T = 1 and H >= 5: Proposition 2.10(a); otherwise mu_s >= T + 1 at every reachable context, shown either by
  "every reachable s context pawn needs >= T + 1 moves" or by a breadth-first search over Q-degree (s's king from its
  square after m, s's context pawns, s-bar's static context pawns) started from every reachable state of each
  component (min over components is a lower bound for mu_s: pawns of other components only hinder). The same
  certificate gives (V2) at the Z-move terminals.
(V2) at passer and race terminals: every terminal reachable by one move from a reachable context, credited by
  cylcgt's rule (count rule for races), must satisfy Corollary 2.11a, i.e. (H1)-(H5) of Proposition 2.11 for some
  pawn p (mu_omega: exact search on the terminal's own component with p deleted, multi-source minimum over the other
  components' reachable states; omega's Z-pawns contribute nothing because the next condition requires they have no
  move). Proposition 2.11 is a win in the frozen model F. It is carried to P by Corollary 2.7' (PROVED in P): every
  king move of omega at the nodes of the push must be a P-loss; this is shown by Theorem 2.7(a) with the strict
  (L1_P)+(L3_F) certificate of that king move (context-independent because Z cannot change: the program requires
  omega to have no Z-pawn move) and (L2) at those nodes by the bound D0 - (e - 1) >= T + 1 (D0 = least number of moves
  of an omega context pawn at the terminal; each omega move lowers it by at most 1). (H2) and (H3) are checked
  statically (Z-pawns next to beta's king file; root files as a superset of occupied files). No terminal: CERTIFIED
  vacuously. Never FAILS (no exact witness is computed). On flat boards the output notes that Proposition 2.11 is
  written out for cylinders only (LOOPY: "the same proof works ... (not separately written out)").
(V4) not checked; needed only for the BIG value S(X) (Theorem 1.14(ii)).
Conclusion ("verdict", "proves", "D_result"):
  - D_result: the D-game of the context alone (Z deleted), chess e.p., first passer or race ends it (credit as
    above), a side with no move loses; memo cap 300,000.
  - CERTIFIED via Theorem 1.14(i) (PROVED) if (V1) [check, (E), (V1b) both sides], (V2) and (V3) [both sides] are
    all CERTIFIED: P(X) = D(X).
  - Else CERTIFIED via the sharp form of Theorem 1.14 (PROVED after repair; its "Reading in the vocabulary of
    Theorem 1.14"), with sigma = the D-winner for the side to move: no check, (V1b) for the loser, (V3) for every
    Z-move of the loser, (V2) at every terminal credited to sigma. Then X is a P-win for sigma. (E) is not needed
    there, because the D used is the whole-board game with chess e.p., whose pawn moves are exactly the legal chess
    context moves.
  - FAILS if separation or a clause fails with a witness and the sharp form does not apply; else UNDECIDED.

THE --ctx ARGUMENTS (this program's, not LOOPY's; off by default)
  (L3) of Definition 2.6 asks for a guided strategy under which the real s is never stalemated in the window.
  Check: on every line of L_T where s-bar plays only winning moves, at every s-turn s has a local move that is
  (i) a Z-pawn move, (ii) a king step onto F_Z, or (iii) a king step onto a square outside U, where U is the union
  over all reachable contexts of the s-bar context pawns' squares and the squares they attack, and of the forward
  cones (on context files only) of s's context pawns. Why it suffices: by Step 1 of the proof of Theorem 2.7 the real
  play projects onto such a line; s-bar's context pawns never move in the window and are only removed, so their
  squares and attacks stay inside U; s's context pawns stay on context files (Lemma 2.2) inside their forward cones;
  so a move of kind (iii) is legal in the real position (empty square, not attacked by a context pawn; the Z part
  and the kings are as in the local position), and (i), (ii) are legal by Lemma 2.2. So s has a legal move, and is
  not stalemated. Only the (V3) certificate uses it; the Corollary 2.7' transfer in (V2) uses the strict T only,
  because after a passer the context leaves the enumerated set.
  (V1b) via a buffer square: a king step from F_Z onto a buffer square that is legal in the Z-only position and not
  attacked by an enemy context pawn in any reachable context (buffer squares never hold a pawn, Lemma 2.2).
  No referee has read these two arguments. Treat --ctx verdicts as COMPUTED-1 at best until one does.

LIMITATIONS (all of them that I know)
  1. Sufficient conditions only. UNDECIDED says nothing about the position; 1,752 of the 1,880 UNDECIDED test
     positions have P = D anyway.
  2. Strict mode cannot certify any trebuchet ((L3_F) fails; LOOPY section 5.1 says so). Certifying them needs (L3)
     in a context, which LOOPY never proves for a context; --ctx does it by an argument of this program that no
     referee has read.
  3. (V2) at passer terminals: Proposition 2.11 (frozen) + Corollary 2.7' only. (H2) fails for every group whose
     king stands next to a Z-pawn file, i.e. every trebuchet, so any reachable terminal credited to that side blocks
     (V2). Theorem 2.11K (real kings) is not used: it also needs (H2). The transfer additionally demands that omega
     has no Z-pawn move. No FAILS is ever reported for (V2).
  4. (V2) on flat boards rests on LOOPY's remark that the cylinder proof of Proposition 2.11 carries over (not
     written out there); the output says so.
  5. (L2) for T >= 2 uses lower bounds on mu_s over an over-approximated reachable set; tight contexts may be
     UNDECIDED although (L2) holds. (L1) is (L1_P) (no queen clause): enough for P (Theorem 2.7), not a chess claim.
  6. (V3) checks every Z-move of the Z-only position, a superset of the moves legal in context (sound, may be
     stricter than needed). The sharp form needs only the loser's moves and is used when (V1)-(V3) do not all hold.
  7. (E): certified only when no usable e.p. right is reachable in a multi-component context; otherwise UNDECIDED
     (LOOPY: needed-ness of (E) is a CONJECTURE). The sharp form does not need (E).
  8. (V4) and (A_Q)(X) are not checked. The chess value needs (A_Q)(X), which is false in general (LOOPY 1.2).
  9. Root with a passer, H < 5, officers, more than the caps (300,000 context states, 300,000 D-game nodes,
     the per-position CPU budget, default 50 s, in the local game): UNDECIDED or FAILS(separation) as stated.
 10. Default F_Z is the shortest arc of the kings' files; a group whose pawns lie outside it needs --fz or --group.
 11. The program is one implementation (COMPUTED-1). It agrees with LOOPY's published T lists and mu_s values and
     with kp_p on every certified test position, but no second implementation or referee has checked it.
 12. Test (b) is a sample (2,080 runs, 5 groups, contexts of <= 2 pawns a side or locked pairs), not a sweep;
     test (a) sampled 30 of the 163 SHARED positions.
