1. TOOLS AND VALIDATION
======================================================================================================================
- bin/kp = kings/kpsolve.c; bin/kpp = K7's kp_p.c (the P model); bin/ref3m = the K23 referee's ref3 (board names pln:7x6,
  cyl:8x6).
- src/kp39.c (binaries kp39 and, after a memory edit, kp39p) = a copy of kpsolve.c with K39 options:
    --refine      capture refinement: a capped promotion by the side the bound favours is scored as the value after the
                  opponent's best capture of the new piece (no capture: the capped terminal stands). Sound for that bound,
                  since restricting the defender to captures can only help the promoter.
    --bound A|B   one bound only;  --capw/--capb per-colour officer caps;  --promw/--promb per-colour promotion sets.
    kp39p additionally keeps solved layers packed (2 bits per state).
  ONE IMPLEMENTATION FAMILY with kpsolve.
- src/own39.cpp (binaries own39 and own39p) = K28's ownref.cpp (from the K27 referee's ownchess.cpp, written from SPEC.md,
  no code shared with kpsolve) extended to the cylinder, the double step and e.p., and move lists. Cap 1, bound A or B,
  refinement on/off. SECOND FAMILY.
- src/own2.cpp = a solver written by K39 (own39's geometry helpers, no kpsolve code): up to two officers, total and
  per-colour caps, per-colour promotion sets, layers solved by un-move retrograde analysis.
- Validation (scripts/cmp.py, scripts/cmp1.py; random positions from scripts/gen_rand.py; outputs logs/r/val*):
  * cap 1, 150 random positions on each of cyl:7x6, cyl:8x6, flat 7x6 (45, 35, 27 illegal; both say X):
    kpsolve vs own39 without refinement, and kp39 --refine vs own39 with refinement: 0 root and 0 move-list differences on
    all three boards (CHECKED-2 for the tools);
  * own2 at cap 1 vs kpsolve on the same 450 positions: 0 differences;
  * cap 2 restricted (A': one officer each, Black promotes only to Q, bound A; B': colours exchanged, bound B), 50 random
    positions with at most 2 pawns on cyl:8x6 (42 legal): kp39 vs own2, 0 root and 0 move-list differences in both;
  * packed binaries (this session, scripts/valp.sh): kp39p --refine and own39p (bounds A, B, refinement) on the 150 flat
    7x6 positions: output byte-identical to the unpacked kp39 and own39 runs, and 0 differences between the two families
    (logs/r/valp_*);
  * NOT_RUN: an unrestricted cap-2 cross-check. kpsolve at cap 2 on the 3-pawn sets passed 3 GB (val2_kpc2_cyl_8x6, killed
    by the watchdog, 861 s); on the <= 2-pawn set it ran out of wall time (val3_kpc2_cyl_8x6, 1,100 s, 2.3 GB); own2
    unrestricted cap 2 was stopped with it (val2_own2c2_*, val3_own2c2_*: no output). The flat 7x6 half of val3.sh never
    started. No unrestricted cap-2 value is claimed below.

  30 deviations: 28 lose, 2 draw.

    (in/ZB_kids_*.in; logs/r/ZBk_kp39_*, logs/r/ZBk_own_*). Black's moves from it: Kb5-a4, a6, b6, c6 and b3-b2 draw,

    resp. "Kb2 kh5 ...": exactly D. White's moves from it: Kb2-a1, b1, c1, c3 and b4-b5 draw, a3-a4 loses. The other wrap
    move Ka4-g3 (h3) loses; from (c3, a4) both wrap moves lose.

  Ka4xa3 from (c3, a4), a3-a4 from (c3, b5)); the other 24 are exact at plain cap 1.
