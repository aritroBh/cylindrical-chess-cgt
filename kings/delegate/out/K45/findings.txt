K45 FINDINGS: is K44's over root an exact over group in P?  (LOOPY Conjecture 4.7, section 8 items 2 and 3)   Task date 2026-11-23
=====================================================================================================================================
AI work, no human re-derivation.  Model P throughout (SPEC.md rules, real kings, e.p. on, promotion pieces QRBN, first promotion wins for
the promoter, stalemate and endless play are draws; Definition 1.5).  Labels: COMPUTED-1 one implementation, CHECKED-2 two independently
written implementations agree.  Nothing here is PROVED except where a known lemma is quoted.  Machine load was high; heavy runs went
through kings/delegate/heavy.sh (at most 2 at once, nice 15).

0. SHORT ANSWER
---------------
(a) K44's over roots are NOT exact over groups of Definition 4.8.  Clause (O4) fails at every freeze of every root (clauses O1, O2, O3
    hold).  Reason: both freezes are non-capturing king moves of White (Kc3-c2 and Kc3-d2); after the freeze White, to move, can play the
    king back (Kc2-c3), reaching the root with Black to move, which Black loses; so q is a White win with either side to move, not a mutual
    zugzwang.  This is the colour mirror of Lemma 4.9 (PROVED), which forbids exactly this.  Same on cyl:11x6 and 12x6, and for the mirror
    (under) group.  The LK value "over" of K44 is an outcome statement against batteries of D-sum contexts; Definition 4.8 is a strictly
    stronger structural condition (q = 0 in P alone).  Neither implies the other.   (CHECKED-2: own solvers A and B, and ref3m and kp_p.)
(b) K44's blocked-pair batteries (one, two or three blocked pairs on distinct files inside an arc of 6 files, pair ranks 2..4, with every
    sub-layer reached by captures and pushes) contain NO exact over or under group on cyl:10x6, 11x6, 12x6, every king placement
    (756 blocked-pair layer lines per width, F4 = 0; CHECKED-2).
(c) BUT exact over and under groups of Definition 4.8 DO exist in plain P, with pawns that are not all blocked pairs.  Conjecture 4.7's
    over half is realised (CHECKED-2; ref3m and kp_p on cyl:8x6, 9x6, 10x6; own solvers A and B on cyl:6x6 to 12x6, 14x6, 16x6).
    The smallest board is cyl:8x6 (none on cyl:6x6, 7x6 in the six file classes searched).  Smallest by pawns: 2 + 3 pawns, on cyl:10x6 only.
    The 3 + 3 group exists on every cyl:Wx6 searched with W = 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 14, 16.
    No exact group with at most 2 + 2 pawns on any three files within six files (all searched widths).
(d) K43's "0 exact over and 0 exact under groups" stands for its scope (pawns on four ADJACENT files).  The new groups have pawns on files a, b, e
    (non-adjacent, a five-file span); K43 and K26 never searched such a set.  K15's five-file range (at most 3 + 2 pawns, cyl:7x6 and 8x6, a different reading of over)
    contains no such group: the 2 + 3 group needs cyl:10x6 and the 3 + 3 group has one pawn more.

1. THE EXACT OVER GROUPS FOUND
------------------------------

    root position   Kf5 kd4 Pb2 Pb4 Pe2 pa4 pb5 pe5 b -        (Black shuttles d4-d5; the White king stays on f5)
    the four roots  (kd4, Black to move), (kd4, White to move), (kd5, Black to move), (kd5, White to move)
    values in P     every root with Black to move is a Black loss, every root with White to move a White win (ref3m, kp_p: W at all four).
    (O1)  Black has 6 moves at the d4 root and 7 at the d5 root; every one of them (the shuttle included) loses at once.
    (O2)  White has exactly one non-losing move at each root, and it is a pawn move, no promotion:
              root d4: e2-e4 (double step; no e.p. right)      root d5: e2-e3
    (O3)  after the freeze Black to move has 4 (resp. 5) legal moves, all lose at once.
    (O4)  after the freeze White to move has 6 legal moves, all lose at once: a mutual zugzwang 0_D.
    So the freezes are pawn moves, as Lemma 4.9 requires, and different at the two roots, as Lemma 4.10 allows.
    Pawn roles: b4/b5 is a blocked pair; e2 and e5 face each other two squares apart (the e-pawn pushes are the freezes); a4 (Black) and b2 (White) are
    free pawns whose pushes lose at once.
    Colour mirror (exact UNDER group): Kd2 kf2 Pa3 Pb2 Pe2 pb3 pb5 pe5 w -, White shuttles d2-d3, Black freezes with pe5-e4 (root d2) / pe5-e3 (root d3).

2. SEARCH AND IMPLEMENTATIONS
-----------------------------
Test: Definition 4.8 for the under group (White shuttles) and its colour mirror, the over group (Black shuttles), restated in K43's findings
2.1 as (O1)..(O4): adjacent pair z of safe squares for the shuttler (K1), the other king on a safe square at distance >= 2 from both (K2), the four roots,
(O1) every non-shuttle move of the shuttler loses at once and the shuttle is legal, (O2) the freezer has a non-losing move at both roots and no
promotion is available, (O3) after every freeze q the shuttler to move has a legal move and all lose at once, (O4) the same q with the freezer to
move (no e.p. flag) is legal and every freezer move loses at once.  "Loses at once" = the position after the move is lost for the mover in P.

3. K44'S ROOTS, CLAUSE BY CLAUSE (item 3)
-----------------------------------------
Position: cyl:10x6, pairs a2/a3, c4/c5, f2/f3 (layer Pa2 Pc4 Pf2 pa3 pc5 pf3), Black shuttles a4-a5, White king c3.  Solver B, diag_K44_over_root_cyl10x6.txt
(and cyl:12x6), counts of moves in P:
    Black to move, ka4: 3 legal moves, all lose at once (shuttle included).     Black to move, ka5: 6 legal moves, all lose at once.        (O1) holds.
    White to move, ka4: 1 legal move that loses at once, two that do not: Kc3-c2, Kc3-d2 (both win).     ka5: Kc3-c2, Kc3-d2 (win).         (O2) holds, no promotion.
    After Kc3-c2 (and Kc3-d2), Black to move: 4 legal moves (ka4) / 7 (ka5), all lose at once.                                          (O3) holds.
    After Kc3-c2, White to move: 6 legal moves at ka4, 7 at ka5; the non-losing ones are Kc2-c3 and Kc2-d2 (both WIN); after Kd2: Kd2-c2, Kd2-c3, Kd2-e3 (WIN).  (O4) FAILS.
    ref3m and kp_p agree: root and every q have result W for either side to move (roots.ref3m, roots.kpp).
So the freeze q has value "White wins whoever moves", not 0.  The colour mirror (a2 c4 h4, under) fails the same way (diag_K44_under_root_cyl10x6.txt).
Why LK's value does not transfer: LK identifies the root with over by outcomes in D-sums with batteries of games (K44, CHECKED-2).  Definition 4.8 asks for
more: that every White option loses at once in P itself, i.e. q is the position 0_D of Definition 4.4.  At K44's roots White's freezing king move keeps a
return move, so q is a positive position (over itself has the White option back to a root).  In the D-model the root's White option need only
have the right outcomes against the battery; in P alone it is not a zugzwang.  Whether K44's roots equal over against contexts IN P was
not tested (the P solvers cannot form a D-sum; see 4).  This is an explanation consistent with the data, not a proof.

4. VALUES BESIDE CONTEXTS (item 2; kp_p --pmodel and ref3m on cyl:10x6; ctx_table.txt)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
P is not additive, so "beside a context" means: add far pawns on files h and i to the root and ask the P solvers.  For the two groups of cyl:10x6
(3 + 3: Kf5 kd4 ...; 2 + 3: Kg4 kf6 ...), 11 contexts x 4 roots x 2 groups = 88 positions, ref3m and kp_p agree on all 88.  Pattern (W/L = White/Black wins):
   context none, White passed pawn Ph5/Ph4/Ph3, blocked pairs (h3/h4, h2/h3, h3/h4 + i2/i3): all four roots W  (the group stays a White win with a White tempo or a zero beside it)
   context Black passed pawn ph2/ph3/ph4: all four roots L  (a Black free tempo beats the group, so over < 1 in these tests)
   exceptions in the 2 + 3 group: Ph5 attacks g6, so the root with the shuttler on g6 and White to move is illegal (X); the race Ph4 + pi4 gives L W L L, the far pawn
   and the White king interact (not additive).  Both solvers agree.  These contexts only sample the order-structure (0 < over < 1); they do not decide
   equality with over.

5. WHAT IS NOT DONE
-------------------
More than three pawn files, files outside a six-file arc (only two seven-file arcs on cyl:10x6), ranks other than six, officers on the board (chess; kpsolve
was not run for the groups), flat boards, W = 13, 15, W > 16 (the 3 + 3 group is expected to persist, by the pattern of W = 9..16), contexts that are D-sums.
That no group exists with fewer than 2 + 3 pawns is a statement about the domain only.  The groups use 6-rank boards; their pawn files a, b, e span five files.
