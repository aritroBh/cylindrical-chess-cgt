K42 FINDINGS: tempo matching, the promotion-free form (LOOPY section 8, open question 1)
Date: 2026-11-22. Model P throughout (first promotion wins for the promoter; real kings; stalemate and infinite
play are draws). s = White makes the sortie, s-bar = Black punishes (Black sorties are colour flips).
AI work (K42 investigator); no referee yet, no human re-derivation.

=====================================================================================================
0. VERDICT
=====================================================================================================
(a) The promotion-free form of tempo matching, as K18 defined it, is FALSE (CHECKED-2: two independent full-board
    P solvers agree on every sampled counterexample; the plain-only flags agree with K18's lg.cpp).
    It fails on all three boards (cyl:7x6, cyl:8x6, cyl:9x6), in every promotion-free context class that was
    tested, and also in K18's own context family (both sides have a context pawn move; e.g. Pe2 Pg2 pe4 pg4,
    the context K18 tested and found punished for its 31 sorties).
(b) Every failure found needs the sortie king: it disappears when s's king is kept off the context files and a
    stalemate of s is scored as a loss for s (COMPUTED-1, exhaustive over all failures of cyl:7x6 and cyl:8x6).
    No "pure tempo" failure (pairing broken while the king stays out of the context) was found.
(c) PROVED (hand proof, PROOF.md): Proposition 8.1' -- for a promotion-free context in which s to move loses the
    pawn game, hypothesis (P2) of Proposition 8.1 is automatic, and (P3) can be weakened from "X and its
    king-neighbourhood" to "X, the squares passed over by double steps (E), and the squares attacked by s-bar's
    context pawns (A)". With (P1), (P1e) and this (P3'), s-bar wins P. On 6,033,423 legal sortie-context pairs
    the certificate of 8.1' holds at 1,213,383 (8.1's at 967,308) and no certified pair is a failure
    (COMPUTED-1 consistency check of the proposition).

=====================================================================================================
1. THE STATEMENT TESTED (brief item 1)
=====================================================================================================
Root Y: king group Z on F_Z = {a, b, c} (Definition 2.1), both kings on F_Z, Z-pawns on ranks 2..5, White to move,
no e.p. right, legal. Sortie m: a legal non-promoting White Z-move (king move anywhere, or Z-pawn move) after which
Black has no immediate promotion. Admissible reply r: a legal non-promoting Black move that leaves Black's king on
F_Z. m is PLAIN-ONLY if some admissible r gives a Black win of the plain local game L^0(Q_Z) and no admissible r
gives a Black win of the pass game L^inf(Q_Z) (Definition 2.6; (L1_P) form; the queen-clause form is recorded
too). Context G: pawns on the context files (cyl:7x6: e, f; cyl:8x6: e, f, g; cyl:9x6: e, f, g, h), at most
2 + 2 pawns, ranks 2..5. G is PROMOTION-FREE if no sequence of legal pawn moves of G alone (both colours, any
order, captures and e.p. captures included) promotes (K18's definition). G is a SECOND-PLAYER WIN FOR THE
PUNISHER if White to move loses the pawn game of G (normal play, e.p. included) -- for a promotion-free G this
is exactly the zero or negative class of `kpsolve --frozen`.

  Promotion-free form (PF-TM): for every such Y, m, G with Y.m + G legal, the position Y.m + G with Black to
  move is a Black win in P.

Reading of "each defender tempo answered by a punisher tempo" (the one proved in 8.1'): Black answers every
White context move by a context move that keeps White to move lost in the pawn game, and every White local move
(king or Z-pawn) by its plain-game strategy sigma; Black never moves a context pawn otherwise.

K18's family required in addition that both sides have a legal pawn move in G; results are given for both the
full set and that family.

2. TOOLS (all in kings/delegate/out/K42/src/)
=====================================================================================================
k42.cpp    own solver, written from kings/SPEC.md and Definition 2.6 (no other solver's code read). Model P by
           pawn-configuration layers with retrograde counting inside a layer. Options turn it into the local game
           (--confine abc: Black's king on F_Z; --pass: White may pass when not in check; --qclause), and the
           diagnostics (--wstalemate-loss, --wconfine FILES, --wbad SQUARES, --p1e).
           Modes: solve, sorties (roots, sorties, plain-only flags), test (sortie x context, P value),
           cert (the certificate of Proposition 8.1 / 8.1').
ctx.py     promotion-free contexts and their pawn-game class (own code).
badsets.py X, E, A and the king-neighbourhood of X for each context (closure of the pawn game under all White
           moves and all winning Black replies).
diag.sh, diagsum.py  re-run every failure under the three diagnostic variants and classify.
cmp_k18.py, k18tag.py, summarize.py, tagfails.py, randpos.py: comparisons and tables.
Second implementations used only as checks: K23's ref3 (full-board P, written from SPEC.md by the K23 referee)
and K18's lg.cpp (local game). Binaries were built in this directory and deleted at the end.

Validation of k42.cpp:
- Full-board P against ref3: 300 random cyl:7x6 positions (0 to 4 pawns a side, 30% with an e.p. right): 0
  differences. 100 sortie-context positions of cyl:8x6 (50 sampled failures, 50 random pairs): 0 differences
  (logs/xcheck8_*.txt). The 6 positions of K18's cx_small.txt: same values as K18's kpp output.
- Local game against lg.cpp: on every root line of lg.cpp's --search output, the set of plain-only sorties is

  output); cyl:7x6 17,055 and 15,370; cyl:9x6 17,123 and 15,428 (lg.cpp run by K42). lg's search lists only
  roots of its own root filter; roots outside it were not compared.
- All 25 plain-only king sorties of K18's ES set occur in k42's sortie list, and against K18's 5 promotion-free
  zero contexts they are punished in k42 too (K18's EP result reproduced).
- Mirror symmetry (reflection of a..c about b, e<->g on cyl:8x6): the mirror contexts c1..c6 / c13..c18 of
  cyl:8x6 give identical L/D/W/X counts.

4. COUNTEREXAMPLES (CHECKED-2: k42 and ref3 agree; plain/pass flags from k42 and lg.cpp agree)
=====================================================================================================
All on cyl:8x6, context G = Pe2 Pg2 pe4 pg4 (K18's family: White e3 or Black e3, White g3 or Black g3 are the
shared one-move gaps; value * + * = 0, promotion-free) unless stated. "bare" = Y.m without G.

(E1) stalemate by a pairing reply. Y = Kb1 Pb2 ka4 pa2 (w), m = Kb1-a1. After m, Black to move.
     Bare: Black wins (only 1...Kb3; then White's only move is Ka1-h2 and ...a1 promotes; plain T = 1, the
     queen-clause T = 2; the pass game is not won: plain-only). With G: DRAW. After 1...Kb3 2.e3! Black's only
     context reply is 2...g3, which attacks h2: White (Ka1; b1 and h1 attacked by pa2, a2 defended) is
     stalemated. Every other Black move at move 1 or 2 loses or draws. Here h2 is in A (attacked by an s-bar
     context pawn in the pairing play) and in R: (P3') fails, as Proposition 8.1' requires.
     ref3: Ka1 Pb2 ka4 pa2 Pe2 Pg2 pe4 pg4 b - | D ; bare | L.
(E2) king raid creating a race. Y = Ka3 Pb3 ka1 pb4 (w), m = Ka3-h4 (plain T = 6 after 1...Kb2, pass game not
     won). With G: WHITE WINS: after 1...Kb2, 2.Kxg4 is White's only winning move (k42 --moves). The context is
     promotion-free only for pawn moves; with the king's capture White's g-pawn can promote in 5 moves (Kxg4, Kh5,
     g2-g4, g5, g6=Q), so mu_s <= 5 < T = 6 and (L2) of Definition 2.6 fails.
     ref3: Kh4 Pb3 ka1 pb4 Pe2 Pg2 pe4 pg4 b - | W ; bare | L.
(E3) the same at a group of K18's NOESC list (plain-only in both the P and the Q variant):
     Y = Kc3 Pa2 kc1 pa4 pa5 (w), m = Kc3-d4 (plain T = 7 or 8 depending on r). With G: WHITE WINS (for example Kxe4 and the
     e-pawn runs; k42 --moves: after 1...Kc1-c2, 2.Kxe4 wins, and so do 2.Ke3, 2.a3, 2.e3, 2.g3). ref3: Kd4 Pa2 Pe2 Pg2 kc1 pa4 pe4 pg4 pa5 b - | W ; bare | L.
(E4) king entry without a race. G = Pe2 Pg2 pe5 pg5 (K18's family). Y = Ka4 Pb3 kb6 pb4 pa5 (w), m = Ka4-h5.
     Bare: Black wins only by 1...Kb5. With G: DRAW. 1...Kb5 now loses (k42: 2.g4 wins for White; 2.Kxg5 does
     not), 1...g5-g3 (the double step) is the only drawing move, and every other move loses. Scoring a stalemate
     of White as a Black win does not change the value; keeping White's king off the context files does
     (variant m2 of section 5), so the king on h5 beside g5 is what breaks the pairing.
     ref3: Kh5 Pb3 Pe2 Pg2 kb6 pb4 pa5 pe5 pg5 b - | D ; bare | L.
(E0) the smallest kind, outside K18's family: G = Pe2 pe3 (blocked, no pawn move for either side, zero class),
     Y = Kb1 kb4 pc2, m = Kb1-c1: DRAW. Bare, Black wins by 1...Kb3, after which White's only move Kc1-d2 loses;
     with G, d2 is attacked by pe3 and 1...Kb3 stalemates White. 6 men (k42; the pattern is that of E1).
Inputs and outputs: data/examples_in.txt, data/examples2_in.txt, logs/examples_*.txt.

5. MECHANISMS (exhaustive over the failures of cyl:7x6 and cyl:8x6; COMPUTED-1, k42 only)
=====================================================================================================
Every failure was re-solved under three variants: m1 = a stalemate of White counts as a Black win; m2 = White's
king may only stand on F_Z and the two buffer files (never on a context file); m3 = both.
  cyl:8x6 (39,673 failures):
    White wins, fixed by m2 alone (king raid; the m1 value stays W)          33,459
    White wins, fixed only by m3 (W under m1, D under m2)                       143
    draws fixed by m1 alone (stalemate by a context pawn's attack)            3,973
    draws fixed by m2 alone (king entry: capture threat, blocking)              437
    draws fixed by either                                                     1,661
    failures that survive m3                                                      0
  cyl:7x6 (15,700): 12,525 W fixed by m2; draws: 2,342 by m1 only, 268 by m2 only, 565 by either; 0 survive m3.
  cyl:9x6: see section 6.
So every failure uses the sortie king: it enters a context file (capture, capture threat, blocking a pawn, or
turning the context into a race), or a context pawn's attack takes away a buffer square and stalemates s. This
matches Proposition 8.1': a failure must violate (P3'). The certificate test confirms it directly: 0 of the
68,395 failures carry an 8.1' certificate (section 7).

=====================================================================================================
7. PROPOSITION 8.1' (brief item 2) -- PROOF.md
=====================================================================================================
PROVED (one derivation): under (PF) (promotion-free, White to move loses the pawn game), (P1), (P1e) and (P3')
[R avoids X, E and A], s-bar wins P. (P2) is dropped (Lemma A: it follows from (PF)); (P3) is weakened. The
proof is the proof of Proposition 8.1 with each use of the king-neighbourhood replaced by X, E or A.
Not done: dropping (P1e) (the lapse-game remedy) and any statement that lets the sortie king into the context.

Certificate check (mode cert, --p1e; a reply r creating an e.p. right at Q is not used):
  board    legal pairs   certified by 8.1'   certified by 8.1   certified failures
  cyl:7x6   1,135,472        192,992            140,831               0
  cyl:8x6   3,195,699        599,649            474,283               0
  cyl:9x6   1,702,252        420,742            352,194               0
  total     6,033,423      1,213,383            967,308               0
(8.1' certifies 246,075 pairs that 8.1 does not.) K18 found (P3) false in all its 1,827 + 248 pairs because its
sorties had T >= 3 and the king could reach the context; with all plain-only sorties many have small T.

8. LABELS AND BOUNDARY
=====================================================================================================
- PF-TM is FALSE: CHECKED-2 for the counterexamples of section 4 and the 50 sampled failures (two full-board
  solvers; plain-only flags in two local solvers). The counts of section 3 are COMPUTED-1 (k42), with the sample
  agreement above.
- Mechanism classification: COMPUTED-1.
- Proposition 8.1': PROVED (one AI derivation, not refereed). Its certificate counts: COMPUTED-1.
- Boundary: model P only (chess not run); F_Z = {a, b, c}; s = White; contexts of at most 2 + 2 pawns on the
  context files; groups of at most 2 + 2 pawns (all), 5 or 6 pawns (cyl:8x6 sample, contexts of <= 3 pawns, section 6).
- A corrected conjecture that keeps the sortie king in play is not offered: the failures show that "promotion-
  free" must at least be strengthened to cover the king's captures (an (L2)-type count with the king) and its
  blocking moves, and that a stalemate hypothesis of (L3)-type is needed. Proposition 8.1' is the proved form.
