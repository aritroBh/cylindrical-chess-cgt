2. THE QUIET-MOVE PROOF (re-derived; full text in EDITS.md EDIT 12)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Verdict: COMPLETE AND CORRECT, with one addition I made explicit. Codex's sketch proves the quiet-move count; it says
"every other Black move freezes a component and supplies a fresh +B" without arguing the sign. The sign needs the same
projection argument (my step 3): if a freeze were credited to Black, the projected D-position P' of C would have a
Black passer (other daughters can only add Black passers through dropped White-owned rights), hence be credited to
Black under every race rule (extra Black passers only shorten Black's distance; mover rules: mover is Black in both),
giving - in tau(C) for tau_P and scored tau alike, against (HSS). With that, steps:
 (0) at Y: + in tau(Z); (HSS) => no C in Y'' has -; (B) at Y => sum_{C in Y''} M_R(C) < B; (E) at Y (Y has >= 2
     components when Y'' is non-empty) => all rights inert, and an inert right also never protects a pawn from the
     passed test (both need the same adjacent enemy pawn) and stays inert while its component is unplayed.
 (1) projected quiet moves are legal in the single global-right D-game of C (file locality; captures only land on
     occupied files; no move uses a right).
 (2) no projected position is terminal: first terminal would have Black passers only (only Black double-step rights
     are dropped by D but kept by F), reached by a Black move => - in tau(C). Contradiction.
 (3) freezes are +B (above).
 (4) count: q <= sum M_R(C) <= B-1; tokens >= (B-q) + f(B-1) >= 1 at each White turn; finite play; Black runs out.
Needs: any-order tau and M (the projected play is Black-only) - YES, needed; stopping at the first terminal - fine,
since the projection passes no terminal before its last step; (B) exactly as LOOPY states it (at every Y, used at the
pre-freeze Y). It does not need the daughter-inheritance argument or M(Y) <= M(X). No hole found.

N(X) alternative (coordinator question (a)). N(X) = most moves of one side in any finite legal sequence from X,
any order, continuing past passers.
 - Under (E): B > N(X) implies (B). At reachable Y, prefix X->Y then concatenate the longest stopped sequences of the
   components of Y (legal by file locality; rights inert by (E), play past passers allowed in N): sum M <= N(X).
 - Converse fails: stopped counts end at the first passer; N continues to the last rank (and N is a root quantity,
   while (B) is per Y). So (B) is the weaker hypothesis; the quiet-move proof needs only (B).
 - The N(X) argument (paper appendix s12a_core.tex A.6) is CORRECT for the count: Black's post-frontier moves,
   freezing ones included, appended to the X->Y prefix, are a legal whole-board sequence, so fewer than B; tokens
   >= B(1+f) - (q+f) >= 1. It still needs a separate argument that later stacks are +B (step 3 above, or the
   daughter-inheritance argument printed in LOOPY after the s12a referee, which K52 calls sound). If A.6 does not
   argue the sign of later freezes, that is a gap there too - I did not read the appendix text.
 - On boards up to 8x8, N(X) <= 8(1+...+6) = 168 < 256, so both bounds are automatic there.
 Mentioned in LOOPY as an alternative (EDIT 12, last bullet).
