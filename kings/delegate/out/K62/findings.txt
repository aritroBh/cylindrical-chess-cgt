SHORT ANSWER
Q1 Ladder length. PROVED. For every N >= 1 there is an abstract exact over group with a bad Black move m such that
   x_m + u_0 (White to move) is a White win beside T_n for n < N and a Black win beside T_N (Proposition 4.21).
   Sharp bound (Theorem 4.20): N*(m) <= |P(x_m) \ E(x_m)| <= b (number of boards), where P = boards whose
   right-free Black-to-move state is reachable from x_m by a White pass in Aux before the freeze, E = those where
   White, to move and not in check, already wins beside -1; sharper N* <= |P \ (E u S_inf)|. N* <= b - 1 when no
   e.p. move of White promotes (always in chess). So T_b (T_{b-1} in chess) replaces T_K, K ~ 4b.
   Attained: N* = b with e.p. promotions (Gamma2(k,AB), b = k + 4 >= 5; Gamma(0,AB), b = 4, in reading M1);
   N* = b - 1 without e.p. (Pi(k,AB), b = k + 5 >= 6; Pi_self(4), b = 5, in M1).
   Smallest boards for N* = N (strict reading: no promotion at any state of a root or q board):
     M1 (K61 model, a move may return to the same board): with e.p. max(4, N); without e.p. max(5, N + 1). Tight.
     M2 (every move changes the board): without e.p. 5, 5, 6, 6, N + 1 (N = 1, 2, 3, 4, >= 5), tight for groups
       without checks (5 boards force N* <= 2); with e.p. 4, 4, 5, 5, N (4 boards for N = 3, 4 open).
   Checks are not needed by any construction; without e.p. every exact group with <= 4 boards satisfies (C), checks
   allowed. Under the literal reading of (O1)-(O4) a 3-board group fails (C) (e.p. promotions at a root board).
Q2 No universal finite family. PROVED (Theorem 4.22). Lambda_N (N + 5 boards, no checks, no e.p.) has the results
   of omega beside every finite D-game with <= N positions, and not beside the short chain C_N (N + 1 positions) or
   T_{N+1}. So every finite set F of finite D-games is passed by Lambda_{max|T|} without it having the results of
   omega; no single finite D-game U (loopy or short) is universal, not even via the root test. {T_N} is universal
   (Theorem 4.18(c)/Corollary 4.19(i); T_b suffices for a group with b boards). The escaping failures are in the
   win half of (C) (D where omega wins); the draw half is decided by under_D alone (K60 remark, not re-derived).
Q3 Fixed boards. PROVED (Corollary 4.23). On a w x h board with <= p pawns a side: T_M, M = (wh)^2 (wh+1)^{2p},
   decides every exact over group (any M >= b - 1). Known real values: Z_O fails at 1...e5-e4 with N* = 2 (root test
   from T_2 at d4); H10, the four cyl:8x7 groups and the five flat 8x7 groups satisfy (B1), so every ladder passes.
Q4 Counter in chess. The counter is a route of Black quiet moves after the bad move on which White's only safe reply
   is a tempo elsewhere (her freeze into a mutual zugzwang lets Black wait elsewhere). In chess: a Black king walk or
   pawn march of n steps that keeps White's freeze available and is dead in every zugzwang the freeze creates.
   Exactness does not forbid it; whether a real group has N* >= 3 is OPEN; search: N* >= 3 at a bad Black root move
   (White wins beside T_2, Aux lost), by the layer recursion on the group's graph.
