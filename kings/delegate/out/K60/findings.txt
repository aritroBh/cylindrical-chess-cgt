K60 findings (2026-11-23). AI work, one agent, not refereed. Details and proofs: PROOF.md.

SHORT ANSWER
- Exact condition (Theorem A, PROVED). An exact over group G has the results of omega beside every finite D-game iff
  (C): for every non-shuttle Black root move m (x_m = position after m, White to move, with White's e.p. right if m is
  a double step), every finite D-game T and position t: r(x_m (+) t, White to move) >= r(omega (+) t, BLACK to move).
  The q's have the results of 0_D, and Black does at least as well beside G as beside omega, with no condition
  (Proposition A0 = K57's proof without its Case E3). In K57's proof (B1) is used only in Case E3 and the token budget
  b = 1 it creates; (C) replaces E3 by "White plays an optimal strategy of G (+) T from the real state".
  Comparing with omega (+) t WHITE to move is sufficient, not necessary (Proposition A2, PROVED, explicit group G2
  with (B1)).
- Decidable on the group's own graph (Theorem B, PROVED). (C) at m iff White wins Aux(x_m) = x_m (+) under_D, White
  to move, sum rules for finite plays, an infinite play a White win iff White passed in under infinitely often (Buchi
  game, 4*|boards| states). Equivalently iff x_m (+) u_0 is a White win in the finite D-game T_N (u_0..u_N, 0; White
  u_i -> u_{i+1}, u_N -> (+); Black u_i -> 0) for every N, or for one N >= K (K = number of (board, e.p., side) states).
  Corollary B3: ONE test game suffices: G has the results of omega iff both roots, Black to move, are White wins beside
  u_0 of T_K. Corollary B2: (B1) = "White wins Aux without passing", which re-proves Lemma 4.15.
- Example (PROVED). G1 (PROOF.md sec. 5, 10 boards, no checks): exact, fails (B1), has the results of omega; after the
  bad Black move White passes first and Black is in zugzwang. K58's witness fails (C) (Aux is a Black win).
- Real groups (COMPUTED-1 / two solvers sharing one move generator). H10: Aux WIN at all 10 bad Black root moves.
  Z_O cyl:8x6: Aux WIN at 10 of 11, LOSS at 1...e5-e4 (d4 root), the only (B1) failure; the K57 referee's vgsum sum
  solver confirms the failure of (C) there: x (+) u_0 is L beside T_2..T_6. So no known real group has the results of
  omega without (B1).

WHAT WAS CHECKED (COMPUTED-1, own code src/k60lib.py, k60games.py, validate.py)
- 7,509 abstract exact toy groups (seeds 11-13; 4,500 random, 3,000 adversarial, plus G1, G2, K58's group three
  times), with checks (4,246 groups), double steps (7,274) and e.p.-only moves (3,067); 282 test D-games each
  (34 named, 96 sums, 150 random loopy with terminals, T_{4n}); 83,127,655 results; 21,556 bad Black root moves.
