**Theorem 4.17 (the exact condition for the results of over). PROVED** (new, K60, 2026-11-23; `kings/delegate/out/K60/PROOF.md` §§2–3; one AI proof, not yet refereed; no human re-derivation). Let G be an exact over group of Definition 4.8, as the P game of its own pieces in a D-sum (as in Lemma 4.15). For a non-shuttle Black root move m let x_m be the position after it (White to move, with White's e.p. right if m is a double step). (a) Each q has the results of 0_D, and r(root ⊞ t, s) ≤ r(ω ⊞ t, s) for White, for every exact group (K57's proof without its Case E3). (b) G has the results of ω beside every finite D-game iff **(C)**: for every such m, finite D-game T and position t, r(x_m ⊞ t, White to move) ≥ r(ω ⊞ t, **Black** to move). Necessity: m is a Black option at the root. Sufficiency: in K57's proof, Case E3 (the only use of (B1), with the token budget b = 1 it creates) is replaced by "White plays an optimal strategy of G ⊞ T from the real state (x_m, t, White to move)". The comparison with ω ⊞ t White to move is sufficient but not necessary (an abstract exact group with (B1) has x_m ⊞ t lost for White where ω ⊞ t, White to move, is won: Black, to move at x_m, would promote). (c) (C) at m holds iff White wins **Aux(x_m)**: x_m ⊞ under_D (under_D = the colour mirror of over_D; White may pass, Black may once freeze, both not in check and clearing the right), White to move, with the D-sum rules for finite plays and an infinite play scored as a White win iff White passed infinitely often (a Büchi game on 4·|boards| states). Equivalently, iff x_m ⊞ u_0, White to move, is a White win in the finite D-game T_N (u_0, …, u_N, 0: White u_i → u_{i+1}, u_N → ⊕; Black u_i → 0) for every N, or for one N ≥ K, K the number of (board, e.p. right, side) states of the group. *Proof idea.* Aux ⇒ (C): White plays Aux's strategy on the board and over's strategy S in T, a virtual Black mirroring White's under-passes as over-passes and S's freeze as under's freeze; S wins, so only finitely many passes happen, and Aux's scoring excludes an infinite board-only tail. (C) ⇒ T_N: ω ⊞ u_0, Black to move, is a White win. T_N (N ≥ K) ⇒ Aux: Black's positional Büchi strategy allows no cycle through a White pass, so fewer than K passes, and it carries over to x_m ⊞ T_N. (B1) is "White wins Aux without passing", so Lemma 4.15 follows again.
