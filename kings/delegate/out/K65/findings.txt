  is 2.69 GB (vgsum59 on C145_18, killed).  Nothing else of the machine was affected as far as I can see, but the 3 GB rule was
  not kept.  All kills were of PIDs that my own watchdog had recorded as descendants of my own heavy.sh (pids.log).
