### Fixes and problems found by the verifier

- REPORT_T35.md does not exist, so the brief's claims table with labels is not delivered. The agent said it could not write one. Fix: write the claims table into the report file. T35 presented 'matches exactly' as validation of T9, but the match reproduces corrupted output. - Inherited ray convention is not stated. RULES.md §5 steps slider rays in the normalised frame, so a diagonal ray reflects off the seam instead of continuing straight in the universal cover. T35's KPK results do not depend on this: with straight-line rays I get the same 126,816 and 216 zugzwangs. T9's Part A conclusions do depend on it. KQK legal count is 98,956 under the literal convention and 106,368 under straight lines. A lone bishop wins (158,852 won) under the literal convention but never wins under straight lines. The double-step choice moves the counts only slightly: 2,423 distinct and 1,194 unique, against 2,419 and 1,190. The passed-pawn test, which can also change values, was not varied. A pawn behind a flipped enemy that could capture it still counts as passed.

It holds only at an equal pawn budget (≤4), and the 'unique' classification is width-relative: only 350 values are unique at both widths. The report should say all this instead of quoting 1,190 and 1,286 as 'new values'.
- About 30% of the w3 positions (55,924 of 187,360) and 38% of the w4 positions (233,500 of 617,088) are excluded from the Möbius value set because both sides have a passed pawn. The summary does not mention this. The cylinder and flat sets are excluded the same way, so the comparison is consistent.

The agent's single-process script is safe, but a chunked or parallel run would inflate the counts. Add a note or a canonical sort.
