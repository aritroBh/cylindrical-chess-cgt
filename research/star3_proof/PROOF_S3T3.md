# $T_n\equiv\star3$ and $S_n\equiv\star3$ against nim heaps, for every even $n\ge12$ resp. odd $n\ge13$ (whole-board game on $C_n$): proof note

**Summary**
1. Theorem (nim-heap outcome equivalence): in the whole-board game on $C_n$ (race rule "count", e.p. on, an e.p.-capturable pawn is not passed, game ends at the first passed pawn), the $(n+1)$-pawn rings $T_n = (B4\,W2)^{(n-10)/2}\,B5\,W2\,W6B7\,W3\,B7\,B6\,W3\,W2\,B6\,W2$ (even $n\ge12$) and $S_n = B4\,B6\,W3\,(W2\,B4)^{(n-11)/2}\,W6B7\,W3\,B7\,B6\,W3\,W2\,B6\,W2$ (odd $n\ge13$) satisfy: for every heap $h<256$, $G+\star h$ has the outcome of $\star3+\star h$ (P for $h=3$, N otherwise). This is written $G\equiv\star3$. It is not a claim of equality of partizan games in standard CGT (section 3, H7).
2. The proof is T50's insertion proof (`research/ring_family_proof/proof.tex`, Lemmas 1-3, with the written-out Lemma 3 of `research/ring_n_plus_2_proof/PROOF.md` 2b), at one fixed cut per family: $T$: $c=4$, $n_0=20$; $S$: $c=7$, $n_0=23$. The $T$ wall is B4/W2 on files $0..n-11$; the $S$ wall is W2/B4 on files $3..n-9$, after the leading $B4\,B6\,W3$. Both are W2/B4 walls in the sense of T50's table; no colour reversal is used.
3. C1: the reduced game at the base has 1305 nodes ($T_{20}$) resp. 40445 nodes ($S_{23}$), no e.p. flag, no passed pawn, zone $\{n-10,n-9\}\cup\{n-7..n-2\}$ resp. $\{0,1,2\}\cup\{n-10,n-9\}\cup\{n-7..n-1\}$, signature NNNPNN ($h=0..5$); files $c-4..c+5$ are exact pattern files in every node; $\mathrm{ins}_c$ maps the graph isomorphically for $n\ge n_0$. C2: every size below $n_0$, i.e. $n=12,14,16,18$ ($T$) and $n=13,15,17,19,21$ ($S$), by direct search. E.p. convention: canonical (a flag only if an enemy pawn stands beside the landing square).
4. Status: PROVED [S] for $n\ge n_0$ given T50's Lemmas 1-3, 2b and the finite checks C1, C2; CHECKED-2 for the base cases. Refereed by two non-Claude models: T71 (Kilo; logic: SOUND WITH FIXES, `research/star3_check_logic/REPORT.md`) and T70 (Codex; SOUND WITH FIXES for the nim-heap claim, with its own independent checker, `research/star3_check/REPORT.md`); fixes applied (section 7). Human check pending.
5. Scope: whole-board game, race rule "count" only, nim-heap addends only; $T_{12}$ and $S_{13}$ are the smallest (the $n=10$ ($T$) and $n=11$ ($S$) members are not $\equiv\star3$).

## 1. Setting

Model, classes T/L/P/K, reduced game: as in `proof.tex` and PROOF.md section 1 (race rule "count"). A **pattern file** is a wall file holding exactly its starting pawn and nothing else. E.p. convention: a flag is recorded only if an enemy pawn stands beside the landing square of the double step; a flag nobody could use is dropped. "No flag" in C1, H4, H6 means this canonical convention.

Files $0..n-1$, $n$ even $\ge12$ ($T$) or odd $\ge13$ ($S$):

- $T_n$: $(B4\,W2)^{(n-10)/2}$ on files $0..n-11$, then $B5$ ($n-10$), $W2$ ($n-9$), $W6B7$ ($n-8$), $W3$ ($n-7$), $B7$ ($n-6$), $B6$ ($n-5$), $W3$ ($n-4$), $W2$ ($n-3$), $B6$ ($n-2$), $W2$ ($n-1$). The tail occupies 10 files and holds 11 pawns ($W6B7$ is two pawns), so the ring has $(n-10)+11=n+1$ pawns. **Wall** = files $0..n-11$ (length $n-10$, even), $B4$ on even files, $W2$ on odd files, $B4$ at the left end and $W2$ at the right end (file $n-11$ is odd since $n$ is even).
- $S_n$: $B4$ ($0$), $B6$ ($1$), $W3$ ($2$), then $(W2\,B4)^{(n-11)/2}$ on files $3..n-9$, then $W6B7$ ($n-8$), $W3$ ($n-7$), $B7$ ($n-6$), $B6$ ($n-5$), $W3$ ($n-4$), $W2$ ($n-3$), $B6$ ($n-2$), $W2$ ($n-1$). The tail occupies 8 files and holds 9 pawns ($W6B7$ is two pawns), so the ring has $3+(n-11)+9=n+1$ pawns. **Wall** = files $3..n-9$ (length $n-11$, even), $W2$ on odd files, $B4$ on even files.

Zone $Z_n$ (the files that change in some node of the reduced game): $Z_n=\{n-10,n-9\}\cup\{n-7..n-2\}$ ($T$), $Z_n=\{0,1,2\}\cup\{n-10,n-9\}\cup\{n-7..n-1\}$ ($S$). For $T$ the zone lies outside the wall. For $S$ the zone contains the leading files $0,1,2$ and the last two wall files $n-10,n-9$; the **fixed run** of $S$ is the wall minus the zone, files $3..n-11$.

## 2. The cut and the base

By C1, in every node of the reduced game of $G_n$ ($n\ge n_0$) all files outside $Z_n$ keep their start content.

- $T$: the whole wall $0..n-11$ is pattern in every node. With $c=4$ the arc $A=\{c-4..c+5\}=\{0..9\}$ lies in the wall iff $n-11\ge9$, i.e. $n\ge20$; it does not fit at $n=18$. Set $n_0=20$. $\mathrm{ins}_4$ fixes files $0..4$ and shifts files $\ge5$ by 2, so $\mathrm{ins}_4(Z_n)=Z_{n+2}$, and $c=4$ is admissible for every even $n\ge20$. Pump: insertion isomorphism $20\to22$ at $c=4$.
- $S$: not every wall file is pattern (the wall-end files $n-10,n-9$ are in the zone); the invariant used is that the fixed arc is pattern. With $c=7$ the arc $A=\{3..12\}$ lies in the fixed run $3..n-11$ iff $n-11\ge12$, i.e. $n\ge23$; at $n=21$ the zone files $11,12$ overlap the arc. Set $n_0=23$. $\mathrm{ins}_7$ fixes files $0..7$ and shifts files $\ge8$ by 2, so $\mathrm{ins}_7(Z_n)=Z_{n+2}$ (at $S_{25}$: $\{0,1,2,15,16,18..24\}$), and the fixed run grows to $3..n-9$ of $S_{n+2}$; the arc stays disjoint from the zone for every odd $n\ge23$. Insertion isomorphisms $23\to25$ and $25\to27$ at $c=7$ are checked by E and R; the pump line for $n=25$ in `pump_S3_13_41.txt` is its check of $23\to25$ against the previous size, at $c=7$.

The ranges are sufficient, not necessary: E's control cuts give exact insertion for $c=5..8$ ($T$, $n=20$) and $c=8..11$ ($S$, $n=23$), first failure at $c=9$ ($T$) and $c=12$ ($S$).

## 3. Hypotheses of Lemmas 2 and 3

**H1 (wall, phase, ends).** $T$: the wall alternates $B4$ (even) / $W2$ (odd), a W2/B4 wall as in T50's table; $\mathrm{ins}_4$ inserts after file 4 copies of files 5 ($W2$) and 4 ($B4$), so the new files 5, 6 continue the phase; $\mathrm{ins}_4(\mathrm{start}_n)=\mathrm{start}_{n+2}$ for $n\ge20$. $S$: the wall alternates $W2$ (odd) / $B4$ (even); $\mathrm{ins}_7$ inserts $B4$, $W2$ on the new files 8, 9, continuing the phase; $\mathrm{ins}_7(\mathrm{start}_n)=\mathrm{start}_{n+2}$ for $n\ge23$. The leading files $0,1,2$ of $S$ are in the zone, outside the arc, and fixed by $\mathrm{ins}_7$. Both walls match T50's table directly (Lemma 2 only needs pattern files $j-1,j,j+1$ of alternating colour two ranks apart, in either phase), so no colour reversal is needed.

**H2 (no capture by $q$).** On a pattern triple, the capture squares of a $W2$ are on rank 3 while the adjacent $B4$ stand on rank 4, and the capture squares of a $B4$ are on rank 3 while the adjacent $W2$ stand on rank 2; so a wall pawn only pushes, and a $B4$ cannot take e.p. a $W2$ that has not moved (PROOF.md H3). After a push an enemy pawn on an adjacent file is still ahead of it; a $W2$ double step to rank 4 beside a $B4$ leaves a live e.p. right and is not passed.

**H3 (windows at the two ends of the arc).** The central pawns are on files $c-3..c+4$; for $j\le c+2$ the window $j-1..j+3$, and for $j\ge c-1$ the window $j-3..j+1$, lies in the arc, which consists of pattern files in every node ($I_n$, H6). Lemma 2 is local to the window, so Lemma 2(b) makes every central move L or P for every $n\ge n_0$.
- $T$, $c=4$: central pawns files $1..8$ at $n_0=20$, files $1..10$ at $n=22$. Left end $j=1$ ($W2$), window $0..4$; right end $j=8$ ($B4$), window $5..9$ (at $n=22$: $j=10$, window $7..11$). E found 0 T/K moves at both ends in all nodes, every central move P (`logs/check_T3_20.log`).
- $S$, $c=7$: central pawns files $4..11$ at $n_0=23$, files $4..13$ at $n=25$. Left end $j=4$ ($B4$), window $3..7$; right end $j=11$ ($W2$), window $8..12$ (at $n=25$: $j=13$, window $10..14$). At the right end the pawn has L moves (28219) as well as P moves (52671); Lemma 2(b) allows L, and there are 0 T/K moves (`logs/check_S3_23.log`). The wall-end files $13,14$ of $S_{23}$ (T and K moves occur there) lie outside the arc and outside both end windows.

**H4 (start).** $\mathrm{start}_n$ has no flag, since no pawn has just double-stepped, and no passed pawn. Every wall pawn is blocked by the opposite-colour pawn on a neighbouring wall file. $T$ core: $B5/W2$, the co-located $W6B7$, then the adjacent pairs $W3/B7$, $B6/W3$, $W2/B6$, and the final $W2$. $S$: the leading $B4$, $B6$, $W3$ are blocked by the seam $W2$ on file $n-1$, the $W3$ on file 2 and the wall $B4$ on file 3; the trailing core after $W6B7$ has the same adjacent-opponent pattern as in $T$. E and R ran the passed-pawn test on all reduced nodes: none has a passer.

**H5 (race rule).** Lemma 2 never uses the race. The insertion shifts files but keeps every pawn's rank and the three-file pattern around the cut, so in Lemma 3 the passed pawns of $c_\mu$ and $\mathrm{ins}_c(c_\mu)$ and their promotion distances coincide; any rule that depends on the passed pawns and on who moved, "count" included, gives the same class (PROOF.md H4). The value is rule-specific, the lemmas are not.

**H6 (invariant).** $I_n$: no node of the reduced game has a flag, and every node agrees with $\mathrm{start}_n$ outside $Z_n$; in particular the arc $A$ is pattern in every node ($T$: the whole wall; $S$: the fixed run $3..n-11$, which contains $A$ for $n\ge23$). $I_{n_0}$ is C1. If $I_n$ holds ($n\ge n_0$): every node is non-terminal with no flag, and $A$ consists of pattern files, so Lemma 3 applies to every node with the chosen $c$; with H4 and induction on the distance from the root, nodes, K-edges and T-flags of $G_{n+2}$ are the $\mathrm{ins}_c$-images of those of $G_n$. An image has no flag; it agrees with $\mathrm{start}_{n+2}$ outside $Z_{n+2}$ since the inserted files are copies of pattern files and $\mathrm{ins}_c(Z_n)=Z_{n+2}$. So $I_{n+2}$. The computed steps ($20\to22\to24$; $23\to25\to27$) agree with this correspondence.

**H7 (meaning of $\equiv\star3$).** As in T50 and PROOF.md H8, and as the theorem is stated: $G\equiv\star3$ means that for every heap $\star h$, $h<256$ (a passed pawn is worth $\pm256$), $G+\star h$ and $\star3+\star h$ have the same outcome: P for $h=3$ and N otherwise. C1 gives signature NNNPNN for $h=0..5$; for $6\le h<256$, the heap move to the P-position $G+\star3$ gives N. The start has no flag and neither do the reduced nodes, so the e.p. convention across a heap move is immaterial (E and R ran both readings "keep" and "expire": identical results). This relation is weaker than equality of partizan games in standard CGT: the numbers 1 and 2 both have outcome L against every nim heap, yet $1\ne2$ (T70). The proof shows neither that the ring games are impartial nor that the nim-heap test determines their standard value, so no standard-value claim is made.

## 5. Proof of the Theorem

Let $n\ge n_0$ be of the right parity. $I_{n_0}$ is C1; H6 gives $I_n$ for all $n\ge n_0$ and $G_n\cong G_{n_0}$ (nodes, K-edges, T-flags). By C1 and Lemma 1, $G_n+\star3$ is a P-position and the signature is NNNPNN as in C1; H7 gives all heaps $h<256$: $G_n\equiv\star3$. For the sizes below $n_0$, $T_{12},T_{14},T_{16},T_{18}$ and $S_{13},S_{15},S_{17},S_{19},S_{21}$, use C2 and H7. $\blacksquare$

## 6. Gaps and caveats

1. **No gap found in the hypotheses.** What is new relative to the $M_n/O_n$ proof is the $(n+1)$-pawn tail with the co-located $W6B7$, the leading $B4\,B6\,W3$ of $S$ (in the zone, fixed by $\mathrm{ins}_7$), and the $S$ wall-end files $n-10,n-9$ in the zone, which is why $S$ uses the fixed-arc invariant and $n_0=23$ rather than the arc-in-wall bound $n\ge21$.
2. **Lemmas 1-3 are cited, not re-proved**; their hand proofs are T50's and 2b, refereed SOUND for $P_n$ (T53, Codex) and $Q_n$ (T62, Codex); 2b was refereed SOUND by Codex (`ring_n_plus_2_check/REPORT.md`). T71 flagged 2b as a dependency; it is not re-proved here.
3. **C1, C2 are computer facts**: the theorem for $n\ge n_0$ is "proved modulo C1"; the sizes below $n_0$ are finite computations. A, E and R agree on every checked position.
4. **Scope**: whole-board game, race rule "count", canonical e.p. convention; race-rule sensitivity and the component-sum model were not examined for $T_n$, $S_n$; $n<n_0$ are covered by C2, not by the induction.
5. **The claim is nim-heap outcome equivalence** (H7), not equality to $\star3$ as a standard partizan game. Do not claim: standard CGT equality, the component-sum model, another race rule, or equivalence against arbitrary addends rather than nim-heaps.
6. **Third program**: the codex `engine.py` run on the smallest base cases planned in the first draft was not made; R (T70's independent program) replaces it and covers every base case.
