# T15: novelty and literature check (web research)

Output: `notes/novelty_check/REPORT.md`.

The project wants to publish results that nobody else has. Search the web carefully: arXiv, Google Scholar, Semantic Scholar, chessvariants.com, talkchess, Integers, the Games of No Chance volumes. For each topic below, report the closest prior work with exact citations and working URLs (no invented references: give only sources you actually opened).

Then give a verdict for each: NEW, PARTLY-KNOWN or KNOWN.

1. CGT (canonical values) of pawn endgames on a cylindrical or toroidal chessboard.
2. Endgame tablebases or mating-material results (KRK, KBBK, KBNK, ...) on cylinder, torus, Möbius or Klein-bottle chessboards. Note the claim by H.G. Muller about the bishop pair on the cylinder: find it and quote it exactly.
3. A value *2 (or *k) in chess positions with only kings and pawns. Cover Elkies 1996/2002, Drummond-Cole 2005 and anything newer.
4. Values 1/8, ↑² (up-second) or tiny(1) realised by chess positions.
5. Validity conditions for "frozen king" or "trébuchet = 0" conventions in chess CGT (Mäser 2002, Siegel's loopy chess work, Gafni 2011).
6. Dawson's chess on a cylinder or ring, and misère Dawson's chess (Plambeck–Siegel).
7. ML/LLM benchmarks built on exact CGT values or game sums.

Also list 5 venues suitable for this work (journals, conferences, student competitions) with their scope and deadlines if known.
