## Background
kings/theory/LOOPY.md proves Proposition 2.11 (the passer certificate). Two Sonnet reviewers checked it and searched for counterexamples in 5,730,596 certified cylinder positions plus 434,429 flat ones. They found one real gap, P11: the statement covered only terminal positions reached by a move, not a starting position that already has a passed pawn. P11 was fixed (commit 1f22f91) so such a root must itself satisfy Prop 2.11. T56 (a separate brief) is the independent proof referee. Your job is the computational one: the passer-root end-to-end sweep, run on this machine as an independent duplicate of a VM run of the same sweep. Any disagreement between your results and the recorded ones is a finding; report it plainly, do not smooth it over.
