\section{Open problems}\label{sec:open}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{$\nim{2}$ with kings and pawns.} Extend the census to width $5$ and to $7$--$8$ pawns, and to
  rings on wider cylinders, under the e.p.-aware test. A $\nim{2}$ at most $3$ files wide could be frozen on the
  $8$-file cylinder without touching the kings' files.
\item \textbf{A certified $1/8$.} Resolve the $8$ decisive end positions of \cref{sec:eighth-real}. A positive
  answer gives a king-and-pawn $8\times8$ position of real oracle value $1/8$, conditional only on the king
  freeze. The freeze is then checkable with \file{kpsolve}-style search.
\item \textbf{The freeze on $8\times8$.} Turn the local certificate of \cref{thm:validity} into an automatic
  proof that a given tr\'ebuchet is $0$ beside a given component.
\item \textbf{New surfaces.} Pawn games and tablebases on the M\"obius strip and the Klein bottle. Pawns
  crossing a twisted seam reverse direction, and no CGT of such boards exists.
\item \textbf{Odd circumference.} On cylinders with an odd number of files, king opposition parity breaks.
  Classify the tr\'ebuchets and the KPK results there.
\item \textbf{King-and-pawn tablebases on the cylinder.} The classic rook-pawn draw disappears there. Give a
  complete KPK and KPKP theory without edges.
\item \textbf{Thermography and atomic weights} of the atlas: which temperatures and atomic weights does the
  cylinder realise?
\item \textbf{Misère Dawson's chess.} Is the misère quotient infinite? What is the misère ring rule?
\item \textbf{Engines and language models on ChessSums} beyond $7$ men, where only CGT knows the answer.
\end{enumerate}
