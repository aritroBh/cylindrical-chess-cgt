\section{Verification and reproducibility}\label{sec:methods}

\paragraph{Evidence labels.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \status{proved}: a written proof. Proofs were drafted with AI assistance. Each was then re-derived
  independently by a second, different model: $8$ of $9$ were confirmed, one after a repair, and one gap was
  found and is reported as a conjecture. A human check is still needed.
\item \status{checked-2}: two independently written programs agree. ``Independent'' means written from the
  rules alone, without reading the other's code.
\item \status{computed-1}: a single implementation.
\item \status{conjecture}: neither proved nor computed.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Cross-checks of the CGT engine.}
\begin{itemize}
\item A brute-force sum solver agrees with \file{cylcgt} on $4{,}967{,}550$ outcome predictions, and on
  $2{,}483{,}775$ more under the e.p.-aware test. These cover $55{,}195$ positions on $3$--$5$ files, three
  race rules, and fifteen reference games $X$ (the prediction is $\mathrm{outcome}(G+X)$).
\item A whole-board minimax agrees on all $1{,}070$ single-component positions tested.
\item The independent C solver of \cref{sec:star2} agrees on all $231{,}071$ census records.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Real chess.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Tablebase.} All positions with at most $7$ men were probed in the Lichess tablebase (Syzygy),
  with every response cached.
\item \textbf{Move generator.} Our legal-move generator matches Lichess's move lists on $160$ of $160$ test
  positions, covering en passant, promotions, pins, checks, mates and stalemates.
\item \textbf{Larger positions.} Positions with $8$ or more men were resolved by exhaustive search with tablebase
  look-ups at every capture.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Use of AI tools.} The software, the searches and the first drafts of several proofs were produced
with AI coding assistants (Claude, Codex and others) under the author's direction. Every headline number has
an exact reproduce command in the accompanying repository, and the label of each claim records how strongly it
was checked.
