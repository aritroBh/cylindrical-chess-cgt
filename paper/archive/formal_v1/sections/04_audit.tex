\section{The 2025 paper, re-audited}\label{sec:audit}

Every pawn-only claim of \citet{Bhattacharjee2025} was recomputed. Each diagram was re-transcribed blind and
compared with a pixel scan. Each verdict below was then checked by three independent reviewers.
\Cref{tab:audit} shows the corrected verdicts. None of them changes when the textbook passed test is replaced
by the e.p.-aware test.
